Manipal University Jaipur (MUJ) Online MBA — Review, Fees and Details

Manipal University Jaipur's online MBA is an option from a UGC-approved university with a NAAC A+ grade, offering 13 specialisations including Analytics and BFSI. It suits learners who want the Manipal name and a choice of specialisations. One note first: this article does not print the MBA fee, because the counsellor guide and the website list different figures for it.

I would rather leave a fee out than print a wrong one. If you are comparing MUJ with other universities, confirm the current MBA total from the university itself before you pay, and compare the other verified fees in this article in the meantime.

What MUJ is

Manipal University Jaipur is a UGC-approved university with a NAAC A+ accreditation, as stated in the counsellor guide. It began offering online degrees in 2021, with UGC approval for the online mode. Its online programmes are designed for fresh graduates and working professionals, and they use a blended approach of theory and practical learning. The Manipal name is one of the most recognised in Indian higher education, and that recognition is the main reason students shortlist MUJ.

The online MBA: what we know

The online MBA is a 2-year programme (4 semesters) with 13 specialisations, including Analytics and BFSI. That is a wider choice than most universities on this site, and it matters: a good specialisation choice can matter more than the university's name. The programme is delivered online with recorded and live learning, semester-wise study and online examinations.

What this article does not do is invent a fee, a ranking or a review score for the MBA. The counsellor guide and the website list different figures for the MBA fee, and because the two sources disagree, no figure is printed as the fee here. That disagreement is exactly why you should always confirm the total in writing from the university before paying — for MUJ, and for every other university too.

Other MUJ fees that are verified

While the MBA fee is held back, the rest of MUJ's fee table agrees across the counsellor guide and the website, and it gives you a fair sense of MUJ's pricing:

  • MCA — ₹1,58,000 total (₹39,500 per semester)
  • BBA — ₹1,35,000 total (₹22,500 per semester)
  • BCA — ₹1,35,000 total (₹22,500 per semester), with specialisations in Data Science and Analytics, Cloud Computing and Cyber Security
  • B.Com — ₹99,000 total (₹16,500 per semester)
  • M.Com — ₹1,08,000 total (₹27,000 per semester)

The guide also notes that Manipal students can avail discounts — a 10% discount on overall fees and 5% on annual payment, plus coupon discounts on semester fees on certain weekdays. Discounts change, so confirm the current offer in writing rather than assuming it.

MUJ vs MAHE: do not confuse the two Manipals

This confuses many students, so let me clear it up. Manipal University Jaipur (MUJ) and Manipal Academy of Higher Education (MAHE) are two different universities under the Manipal umbrella. MAHE offers an MBA for Working Professionals at ₹2,80,000 total, an MCA at ₹2,20,000 total, and MSc programmes in Data Science and Business Analytics at ₹2,80,000 total. MUJ is the Jaipur university; MAHE is the older Manipal institution. They have different programmes, different fees and different admission portals. Make sure you are applying to the one you actually researched.

Who should choose MUJ

Choose MUJ's online MBA if you want the Manipal brand, you want a wide choice of specialisations, and the confirmed fee fits your budget. Look elsewhere if the confirmed fee stretches your budget — Mizoram University (₹50,700–₹53,100), SMU (₹1,10,000) or JAIN Online (₹98,000–₹1,40,000 per year) offer the same UGC-entitled degree value for less. And as with every university, check the UGC-DEB entitlement before paying, because only a UGC-approved university's online degree equals a regular degree.
