How to Do an Online Degree — Step-by-Step Guide

To do an online degree in India, pick a UGC-DEB approved university, choose your course, apply on the university's website with your documents, pay through the portal, and study through live and recorded classes with online exams. A bachelor's degree takes 3 years and a master's takes 2 years, and it equals a regular degree when the university is UGC-approved.

That is the whole process in one paragraph — but the mistakes happen in the details: choosing the wrong university, paying an agent, or picking a course that does not match your goal. This guide walks you through every step the right way.

Step 1: Decide what you want the degree for

Before you look at a single university, answer this honestly: why do you want this degree?

  • A first degree after Class 12: you need a bachelor's — BBA, BCA, B.Com or BA — 3 years. Choose by the work you enjoy: business (BBA), computers (BCA), finance (B.Com), or arts (BA).
  • A master's after graduation: you need a 2-year PG degree — MBA, MCA, M.Com or MA. The MBA is the most popular for working professionals; the MCA is the tech route after BCA.
  • A promotion or career change while working: an online MBA or MCA done alongside your job is usually the answer.
  • A low-cost, recognised degree: budget matters. Mizoram University's online MBA is ₹50,700–₹53,100 in total, its BBA is ₹55,700 and its BCA is ₹64,700 — the lowest genuine fees on the site.

Write your goal down in one line. If you cannot, do not apply yet — a degree chosen without a goal is the most common reason students drop out.

Step 2: Choose a UGC-approved university

This is the step that protects you from fraud. Only a UGC-DEB approved university's online degree equals a regular degree. Before you trust any university:

  • Check it on the UGC-DEB list of approved online universities. If it is not there, do not enrol, no matter how good the offer sounds.
  • Prefer universities you can verify independently. The 17 universities on FindRightCollege — including NMIMS CDOE, Amity Online, Sikkim Manipal University, UPES Online, Dr. D.Y. Patil Pune, Mizoram University and Jamia Hamdard — are UGC-approved for their online programmes.
  • Never trust an "admission agent" who offers a seat for a fee. Real universities take applications on their own official portals.

Step 3: Compare fees honestly

Compare the total fee, not the first instalment. Ask for the full programme fee in writing, including registration and exam charges. Per-semester payment, like Jamia Hamdard's online MBA at ₹23,750 per semester and its online B.Com at ₹9,000 per semester, is much easier on a family budget than a single large payment.

Step 4: Check eligibility and apply on the official website

Eligibility is straightforward:

  • Bachelor's degrees (BBA, BCA, B.Com, BA): you need to have passed Class 12 (10+2) from a recognised board.
  • Master's degrees (MBA, MCA, M.Com, MA): you need a bachelor's degree in any discipline from a recognised university, usually with around 50% marks. Most online MBAs do not require an entrance exam.

Apply only on the university's official website: create an account, fill in your details, choose your programme, and upload your documents — Class 10 and 12 certificates, graduation marksheet and certificate (for PG courses), a government ID, and photographs. Fill everything yourself, slowly.

Step 5: Pay the fee — only through the official portal

Pay only through the university's official payment link, and keep the receipt. Never pay to a personal bank account, a UPI ID sent on WhatsApp, or an "agent" who promises a discount for paying offline. If the fee is large, ask whether semester-wise payment is available — it usually is.

Step 6: Study, submit, and sit for exams

This is where the degree is actually earned:

  • Classes: a mix of live sessions and recorded lectures — attend the live ones when you can, and use recordings to revise.
  • Assignments: each subject has assignments with deadlines — start them early.
  • Exams: conducted online, usually with remote proctoring (your webcam and screen are monitored). You will need a stable internet connection and a working laptop on exam day.
  • Time needed: plan for 8–10 focused hours a week — a fixed daily slot beats an irregular 10-hour weekend.

Step 7: Get your degree and use it

After your final semester and exams, the university issues your degree certificate — valid for private jobs, government jobs and higher education, exactly like a regular degree. Use it with a skill: one project, one internship, one certification in a real tool.

Frequently Asked Questions

How can I do an online degree?

Pick a UGC-DEB approved university, choose your course, apply on the university's official website, pay through the official portal, and study through the university's learning platform. A bachelor's degree takes 3 years and a master's takes 2 years. Never apply through an agent.

Is an online degree valid in India?

Yes — an online degree from a UGC-DEB approved university is valid and equals a regular degree. The university must be UGC-approved for online programmes, so always verify it on the UGC-DEB list before enrolling.

How much does an online degree cost?

It varies widely. Online MBA fees on the FindRightCollege list range from Mizoram University's ₹50,700–₹53,100 to around ₹1,99,000 at Amity Online. Undergraduate degrees are cheaper — Mizoram's BBA is ₹55,700 and BCA is ₹64,700. Always confirm the total fee, including registration and exam charges, in writing before you pay.

Can I do an online degree while working?

Yes, that is exactly what online degrees are designed for. Lectures are usually recorded or held in the evenings, and exams are conducted online. Most working students manage with 8–10 hours a week, without leaving their jobs.

Ask Your Senior

Doing a degree online is not the easy option — it is the possible option for students who cannot move to a big city or quit their job. It works only if you treat it like a real degree: fixed hours, honest study, assignments on time, exams taken seriously. Choose a UGC-approved university, pay only on the official website, and show up for yourself every week for 2 or 3 years. That is the whole secret.
