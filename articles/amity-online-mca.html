Amity University Online MCA — Course, Fees and Details

Amity University Online offers its online MCA for ₹1,70,000 in total (₹42,500 per semester over 4 semesters). It is a 2-year postgraduate degree in computer applications, done fully online after graduation, with industry-linked variants in machine learning, AR/VR, software engineering and cyber security. The degree is valid only if the university holds UGC approval for online programmes.

You have graduated, you want an MCA, and Amity's name is everywhere. The name is real, but the decision should not be made on hoardings. This article gives you the fee, the subjects and the variants, so you decide with facts.

Amity Online MCA at a Glance

  • University: Amity University Online, Noida
  • Degree: Online MCA (Master of Computer Applications)
  • Duration: 2 years, 4 semesters
  • Total fee: ₹1,70,000 (₹42,500 per semester)
  • Eligibility: A bachelor's degree is the standard requirement for the online MCA programmes listed on FindRightCollege. JAIN's brochure specifies a bachelor's degree with 50%. Confirm Amity's exact criteria with your college senior before you apply — eligibility is never guessed here.
  • Mode: Fully online — recorded lectures, live sessions, online exams and digital study material

Amity Online lists 31 online programmes, one of the widest ranges on this site. A wide range is useful, but it is not a reason to choose a university. Choose on fee, subjects and what you will actually learn.

What You Study in Amity Online MCA

The regular Amity online MCA covers advanced programming, data structures and algorithms, database management systems, operating systems, computer networks, software engineering and web technologies, with projects in the later semesters.

Amity also offers industry-linked MCA variants with TCS iON and HCLTech, including Machine Learning, AR/VR, Software Engineering and Cyber Security. These variants carry the company's name and add industry-flavoured subjects and certifications to the degree. They cost more than the regular MCA, and the exact fee for your variant should be confirmed before admission, because variant fees change and should never be assumed from the regular MCA fee.

Pick a variant for its subjects, not its logo — recruiters still test what you can do.

Amity Online MCA Fees — and How They Compare

The regular Amity online MCA fee is ₹1,70,000 in total, paid as ₹42,500 per semester. Amity also publishes a discounted one-time fee option, so ask your college senior what the one-time payment figure is on the day you apply, and compare it with the semester-wise total before deciding.

For comparison, here are other online MCA fees that pass the same check on FindRightCollege:

  • Shoolini University MCA: ₹82,000 total
  • SMU (Sikkim Manipal) MCA: ₹98,000 total
  • MUJ (Manipal University Jaipur) MCA: ₹1,58,000 total (₹39,500 per semester)
  • UPES Online MCA: ₹1,70,000 total
  • MAHE (Manipal Academy of Higher Education) MCA: ₹2,20,000 total (₹55,000 per semester)
  • JAIN Online MCA: ₹75,000–₹1,10,000 per year by specialization, plus a ₹2,500 one-time registration fee and a ₹3,000 yearly examination fee
  • Jamia Hamdard MCA: ₹20,250 per semester

Amity sits at the higher end, level with UPES and above MUJ. The question to ask is simple: what are you getting for the extra money? If the answer is a variant with subjects you genuinely want, it may be worth it. If the answer is only the brand name, think twice.

How Admission Works

Admission follows the standard online-degree steps: choose your university, course and specialization; check the eligibility and keep your documents ready; fill the application form; upload your documents; and pay the fee on the official payment portal. Your graduation mark sheets and degree or provisional certificate, a government photo ID, recent photographs and your signature are the usual documents.

Amity's admission team may call you after you enquire. That is normal. What is not normal is pressure — "seat is filling, pay today" is a sales line, not a deadline. A real admission window gives you time to compare and decide.

Is an Online MCA from Amity Valid?

An online degree is treated at par with a regular degree only when the university is UGC-approved to offer that programme online. That is the condition, every time, for every university. Before paying, check Amity's UGC-DEB entitlement for the MCA programme on the UGC-DEB portal yourself. Validity is a fact you verify, not a promise you accept on a phone call.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the total fee of Amity University online MCA?

The regular online MCA costs ₹1,70,000 in total, paid as ₹42,500 per semester over 4 semesters. Industry-linked variants with TCS iON and HCLTech cost more, and their exact fee should be confirmed before admission. A discounted one-time payment option is also published, so compare both payment routes before you pay.

What is the duration of the online MCA from Amity?

It is a 2-year degree spread over 4 semesters. All online MCA programmes listed on FindRightCollege follow this 2-year structure. If you are working alongside, plan your weeks around the semester schedule rather than trying to finish everything at the last minute.

Who can apply for Amity online MCA?

Graduates with a bachelor's degree can apply for an online MCA. That is the standard eligibility across the programmes listed here, and JAIN's brochure specifies a bachelor's degree with 50%. Amity's exact criteria — including any mathematics requirement — should be confirmed with your college senior before you fill the form.

Is Amity online MCA good for jobs?

It can be, but no degree guarantees a job. An online MCA from a UGC-approved university makes you eligible for the same entry-level IT roles as a regular MCA graduate, and hiring depends on your coding skill, your projects and your interview.

Ask Your Senior

Amity's online MCA is a solid, well-known option, but it is also one of the pricier MCAs on this list. Before you pay ₹1,70,000, compare it honestly with MUJ at ₹1,58,000, SMU at ₹98,000 and Shoolini at ₹82,000, and ask what the difference is buying you. If you want the TCS iON or HCLTech variant for its subjects, go for it with open eyes. If you only want a good MCA degree, the cheaper UGC-approved options will take you to the same interviews.
