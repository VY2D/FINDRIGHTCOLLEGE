Online Degree Courses in Maharashtra — Universities You Can Study From

Maharashtra is home to three of the 17 universities on FindRightCollege — Dr. D.Y. Patil University in Pune, Symbiosis International in Pune, and NMIMS in Mumbai — and you can study from all three fully online, from anywhere in the state. Their verified online fees run from ₹1,20,200 for a D.Y. Patil BBA to ₹3,70,000 for a Symbiosis MBA.

You live in Maharashtra — maybe in Mumbai, Pune, Nagpur, Nashik, or a smaller town — and you want a degree from a university based in your own state. That is a sensible preference: a local university feels closer, its name is recognised by local employers, and you can reach its campus if you ever need to. This guide covers the three Maharashtra-based universities on FindRightCollege, their verified courses and fees, and the honest reminder that online study makes the whole country your campus.

Dr. D.Y. Patil University, Pune — the budget pick

Dr. D.Y. Patil University is a deemed-to-be university in Pune, NAAC rated A++, and its online division is one of the most affordable among the well-known names.

  • Online MBA: ₹1,89,400 total for Indian civilian students.
  • Online BBA: ₹1,20,200 total — the lowest-fee online BBA among the well-known private universities on FindRightCollege.
  • Certificate programmes: ₹28,000 per certificate — short, focused credentials in areas like Digital Marketing and Hospital and Healthcare Management.
  • BBA specialisations: Marketing, Finance, HR, IT and Systems, Retail, International Business, Banking and Financial Services, and E-Commerce.
  • Best for: Budget-first students in Maharashtra who want a recognised Pune university's degree without financial strain.

Symbiosis International, Pune — the premium pick

Symbiosis is one of the most recognised names in Indian management education, and its online school (SSODL) carries that brand into online degrees.

  • Online MBA: ₹3,70,000 total (₹3,15,000 with the work-experience scholarship) — a dual-specialisation programme, and the most expensive online MBA on FindRightCollege.
  • Online BBA: ₹1,65,000 total (₹27,500 per semester).
  • Online BCA: ₹1,57,500 total (₹26,250 per semester).
  • MSc Data Science: ₹1,94,400 total (₹48,600 per semester).
  • PG Diplomas: From ₹19,000 total — short, affordable Symbiosis credentials for working professionals.
  • Best for: Students who specifically want the Symbiosis brand and a dual-specialisation MBA, and whose families can afford the premium fee comfortably.

NMIMS, Mumbai — the management brand

NMIMS (Narsee Monjee Institute of Management Studies) is based in Mumbai and is one of the strongest management brands in India. Its online programmes run through NMIMS CDOE.

  • Online MBA: ₹1,96,000 total, plus a ₹1,200 processing fee.
  • Online BBA: ₹1,31,000–₹1,45,000 total, depending on the specialisation (₹1,31,000 for Marketing and Finance, up to ₹1,45,000 for Business Analytics).
  • Online B.Com: ₹94,000 total, plus a ₹1,200 registration fee.
  • Executive MBA (WX) for Working Executives: ₹4,00,000 total — for senior professionals with significant work experience.
  • Best for: Students in Mumbai and across Maharashtra who want a strong management brand at a mid-range fee.

You are not limited to Maharashtra

Here is the part most students miss: an online degree does not care where you live. A student in Pune can study from Manipal University Jaipur, Amity, LPU, or Mizoram University just as easily as from a Pune university — the classes, exams, and degree are all online. Some verified options from outside Maharashtra:

  • Mizoram University Online MBA: ₹50,700–₹53,100 total — the lowest verified MBA fee in India.
  • Sikkim Manipal (SMU) MBA with Dual Specialisation: ₹1,10,000 total.
  • Manipal University Jaipur (MUJ) BBA and BCA: ₹1,35,000 each.
  • Amity Online BBA: ₹1,65,000; Amity Online MBA: ₹1,99,000.
  • LPU Online MBA: ₹1,61,600 with the Student Grant; LPU Online BBA: ₹1,22,400 with the grant.

So use the Maharashtra universities as your shortlist, not your boundary. Compare them with the national options on fee, specialisation, and format — and then choose.

What to check before you apply

First, verify the university's UGC-DEB entitlement on the UGC website — this is the condition that makes an online degree equal to a regular degree, and it matters for every university, local or not. Second, compare total fees, not first-semester figures, and check whether any grant or discount in the price is permanent. Third, confirm whether classes are live or recorded, and how exams are conducted. Fourth, read the refund policy before paying.

Frequently Asked Questions

Which universities offer online degrees in Maharashtra?

Three of the 17 universities on FindRightCollege are based in Maharashtra: Dr. D.Y. Patil University in Pune, Symbiosis International in Pune, and NMIMS in Mumbai. All three offer UGC-entitled online degrees — MBA, BBA, BCA, B.Com, and more — that you can study fully online from anywhere in the state.

Which is the cheapest online degree in Maharashtra?

Among verified fees, Dr. D.Y. Patil's online BBA at ₹1,20,200 total is the cheapest full degree from a Maharashtra-based university, followed by NMIMS's B.Com at ₹94,000. D.Y. Patil's certificate programmes at ₹28,000 are cheaper still, but they are short certificates, not full degrees.

Can I study from a university outside Maharashtra while living in Maharashtra?

Yes. Online degrees are location-free — a student in Maharashtra can enrol in any UGC-entitled online programme in India, including Manipal University Jaipur, Amity, LPU, Mizoram University, and Sikkim Manipal, and study entirely from home. Your location does not limit your options; your goal and budget should decide them.

Is an online degree from a Maharashtra university valid?

Yes, provided the university is UGC-entitled to offer online degrees. An online degree from D.Y. Patil, Symbiosis, or NMIMS is valid for private-sector jobs, government exams, and higher studies on the same terms as a regular degree from a UGC-approved university. Verify the university on the UGC-DEB website yourself before admission.
