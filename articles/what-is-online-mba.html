What Is an Online MBA? Full Course Details Explained

An online MBA is a 2-year Master of Business Administration degree (4 semesters) that you complete entirely online — through recorded lectures, live sessions, assignments and semester exams — from a UGC-entitled university. It teaches management, marketing, finance, HR, operations and analytics, and it holds the same value as a regular MBA when the university is UGC-approved.

If you have just finished graduation, or you are working and thinking about the next step, this is probably the degree you keep hearing about. Let me explain it properly — what it is, what you actually study, and what it is not.

How an online MBA works

You enrol with a university, and you get access to its online learning platform. You watch recorded lectures when it suits you, join live sessions (usually on weekends), read study material, submit assignments, and appear for a term-end examination every semester. You do not attend a physical campus, and you do not relocate. The programme runs for 2 years across 4 semesters, and you study alongside your job or other commitments. Some universities also offer campus immersion or networking events, but these are optional extras, not the core of the degree.

What you study

The first year usually covers the foundations of management: organisational behaviour, marketing management, financial management, human resource management, operations and business communication. In the second year, you choose a specialisation and study it in depth. The common specialisations are Marketing, Finance, Human Resources, Operations and Business Analytics. Niche options also exist: UPES offers Oil and Gas Management and Power Management, Amity offers a Hospital and Healthcare MBA, and SMU offers a dual-specialisation MBA where you study two subjects in one degree. Your specialisation choice shapes your career more than the university's name, so choose it by the job you want.

Who can do it

Generally, you need a bachelor's degree (10+2+3 or equivalent) from a recognised university, usually with a minimum of 50% marks. Some universities relax this for candidates with work experience, and final-year graduation students can also apply at several universities. There is no entrance exam for the online MBA programmes listed on this site. An Executive MBA is different: for example, NMIMS's Executive MBA (WX) requires a bachelor's degree with a minimum of 55% and 3+ years of work experience, and it costs ₹4,00,000. Do not confuse the two — a regular online MBA is open to fresh graduates, while an executive MBA is built for senior professionals.

Is it valid?

Yes — with one condition that you must never skip. An online degree from a UGC-approved university is treated at par with a regular degree. That is what makes it valid for jobs, promotions, government recruitment and higher studies. But the condition is the whole point: check that the university is entitled by UGC-DEB to offer the programme online, on the official UGC-DEB list, before you pay any fee. A degree from a university without that entitlement is not the same thing.

What it costs

Total fees on this site run from ₹50,700 (Mizoram University) to ₹4,00,000 (the NMIMS Executive MBA). Most standard online MBAs fall between about ₹1,10,000 and ₹2,20,000 in total:

  • Jamia Hamdard — ₹23,750 per semester
  • Sikkim Manipal University (SMU) — ₹1,10,000 total, dual specialisation
  • JAIN Online — ₹98,000–₹1,40,000 per year
  • Chandigarh University Online — ₹1,58,000 with Early Bird Discount; ₹2,10,668 without it
  • LPU Online — ₹1,61,600, with the current 20% Student Grant as published on its official programme pages
  • NMIMS CDOE — ₹1,96,000 total
  • Amity University Online — ₹1,99,000 total
  • Dr. D.Y. Patil Pune — ₹1,89,400 total
  • UPES Online — ₹1,75,000–₹2,20,000 by specialisation
  • MAHE — MBA for Working Professionals, ₹2,80,000 total
  • Symbiosis Online — ₹3,70,000 total (₹3,15,000 with the work-experience scholarship)

Always compare the full programme cost, including registration and examination charges, and ask whether the quoted fee is an instalment total or a discounted one-time payment. The fee of Manipal University Jaipur's MBA is not printed here because the counsellor guide and the website list different figures for it.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is an online MBA programme?

An online MBA is a 2-year postgraduate management degree completed entirely online, through recorded lectures, live sessions, assignments and semester exams. It covers management foundations in the first year and a specialisation — such as Marketing, Finance, HR or Business Analytics — in the second year. From a UGC-entitled university, it holds the same value as a regular MBA.

What is the duration of an online MBA?

An online MBA takes 2 years, divided into 4 semesters. Each semester includes internal assessments or assignments and a term-end examination. You study alongside your job or other commitments, and the degree is awarded after you complete all four semesters.

Is an online MBA equal to a regular MBA?

Yes, provided it is from a UGC-approved university. An online degree from a UGC-entitled university is treated at par with a regular degree, and it is valid for jobs, promotions, government recruitment and higher studies. The learning mode is different — online instead of on campus — but the degree value is the same.

What is the difference between an online MBA and an executive MBA?

A regular online MBA is open to fresh graduates and working professionals, and it teaches management foundations plus a specialisation. An executive MBA, such as NMIMS's MBA (WX) at ₹4,00,000, is built for senior professionals with 3+ years of experience and focuses on strategic, experience-based management. Choose the executive version only if you already have significant work experience.
