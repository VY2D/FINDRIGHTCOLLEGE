Manipal University Online BCA — Course, Fees and Details

Manipal University Jaipur (MUJ) offers its online BCA for ₹1,35,000 in total (₹22,500 per semester over 6 semesters). It is a 3-year undergraduate degree in computer applications, done fully online after Class 12, with specializations in Data Science & Analytics, Cloud Computing and Cyber Security. The degree is valid only if the university holds UGC approval for online programmes.

You finished Class 12, you like computers, and everyone around you has a different opinion. One relative says BCA has no value, another says only B.Tech matters, and a coaching centre is already calling you daily. You need the real fee, the real subjects and an honest answer.

Manipal Online BCA at a Glance

  • University: Manipal University Jaipur (MUJ), Jaipur
  • Degree: Online BCA (Bachelor of Computer Applications)
  • Duration: 3 years, 6 semesters
  • Total fee: ₹1,35,000 (₹22,500 per semester)
  • Eligibility: 10+2 pass from a recognised board is the standard requirement for the online BCA programmes listed on FindRightCollege. Confirm MUJ's exact criteria with your college senior before you apply — eligibility is never guessed here.
  • Mode: Fully online — recorded lectures, live sessions, online exams and digital study material

MUJ is NAAC A+ accredited, and its online degrees started in 2021 with UGC approval. That history matters more than any advertisement. A degree's value comes from the university behind it, not from the website selling it.

What You Study in Manipal Online BCA

MUJ lists three specializations for its online BCA: Data Science & Analytics, Cloud Computing and Cyber Security. You pick one based on where you want to go, not based on which word sounds most impressive.

Across the six semesters, a BCA typically covers programming, data structures, database management systems, operating systems, computer networks, web development and software engineering, with projects in the later semesters. Here is the honest part most sellers skip: the specialization on your degree does not get you a job by itself. What gets you interviews is what you build during those three years — small projects, a GitHub profile, and the ability to explain your own code in an interview.

Manipal Online BCA Fees — and How They Compare

The MUJ online BCA fee is ₹1,35,000 in total, paid as ₹22,500 per semester. There is no separate headline fee and hidden total here — the per-semester figure and the total agree.

For comparison, here are other online BCA fees that pass the same check on FindRightCollege:

  • JAIN Online BCA: ₹45,000 per year (₹1,35,000 total), plus a ₹2,500 one-time registration fee and a ₹3,000 yearly examination fee
  • Shoolini University BCA: ₹78,000 total
  • Amity University Online BCA: ₹1,50,000 total (₹25,000 per semester)
  • UPES Online BCA: ₹1,50,000 total
  • Jamia Hamdard BCA: ₹16,000 per semester
  • Mizoram University BCA: ₹64,700 total

MUJ sits in the middle of this range. It is not the cheapest option — Shoolini and Mizoram cost less — and it is not the most expensive. If budget is your main worry, say so honestly before choosing. A cheaper degree from a UGC-approved university plus strong self-learning beats an expensive degree with no skills every single time.

How Admission Works

Admission to an online BCA follows the same five steps across the universities listed here: choose your university, course and specialization; check the eligibility and keep your documents ready; fill the application form; upload your documents; and pay the fee on the official payment portal. Your 10th and 12th mark sheets and certificates, a government photo ID, recent photographs and your signature are the usual documents.

Pay only on the official portal, and keep every receipt. If anyone asks you to pay a "blocking amount" to a personal account, stop and ask us first.

Is an Online BCA from Manipal Valid?

An online degree is treated at par with a regular degree only when the university is UGC-approved to offer that programme online. That is the condition, every time, for every university. Before paying, check the university's UGC-DEB entitlement for the BCA programme, and verify it on the UGC-DEB portal yourself. A degree from a university that is not entitled for online delivery is not a bargain at any price.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the total fee of Manipal University online BCA?

The total fee is ₹1,35,000, paid as ₹22,500 per semester over 6 semesters. This is the fee for Manipal University Jaipur (MUJ), the Manipal university that offers the online BCA. MAHE Manipal, the other Manipal university on this site, offers postgraduate online programmes and does not offer an online BCA.

What is the duration of the online BCA from Manipal?

It is a 3-year degree spread over 6 semesters. Online BCA programmes listed on FindRightCollege follow the same 3-year structure, so plan for three years of study alongside whatever else you are doing — a job, a family business, or preparation for something else.

Who can apply for Manipal online BCA?

Students who have passed 10+2 from a recognised board can apply for an online BCA. That is the standard eligibility across the online BCA programmes listed here. MUJ's exact criteria for your board and stream should be confirmed with your college senior before you fill the form, because a wrong assumption at this stage costs a full semester.

Is an online BCA from Manipal good for jobs?

It can be, but the degree alone does not guarantee a job. An online BCA from a UGC-approved university makes you eligible for the same entry-level IT roles as a regular BCA graduate, and hiring depends on your programming skill, your projects and your interview.

Ask Your Senior

If you like computers and you want a degree without leaving your town or your job, MUJ's online BCA at ₹1,35,000 is a fair, middle-of-the-road option. But do not choose it because of the Manipal name alone. Compare it with Shoolini at ₹78,000 and Mizoram at ₹64,700, ask yourself what the extra money is buying you, and then decide with a cool head. The name on the degree matters less than the code you can write when the interviewer asks you to.
