Cheapest Online MBA in India — Genuine Low-Fee Options Compared

The cheapest genuine online MBA in India from a UGC-approved university is Mizoram University's, at ₹50,700–₹53,100 in total depending on specialisation. The next cheapest on the FindRightCollege list is Sikkim Manipal University's dual-specialisation MBA at ₹1,10,000. Both are real, recognised degrees — but "cheapest" is only half the question; the other half is what you actually get for the fee.

Every budget search starts the same way: a student types "cheapest MBA" and finds prices from ₹20,000 to ₹4,00,000, plus ads promising MBAs for the price of a phone. The gap is so wide that it feels like a trick. It is not a trick — but the cheapest option is not always the cheapest deal, and some very low prices are not real degrees at all. This article compares only genuine, UGC-approved options from the 17 universities on FindRightCollege, with real brochure fees.

The genuine low-fee list

The lowest total fees for a full 2-year online MBA on the site, from UGC-approved universities:

  • Mizoram University — ₹50,700–₹53,100 total. A central university (set up under the UGC by the Government of India), and the cheapest real MBA on the list by a large margin. General, Finance, Marketing and Entrepreneurship cost ₹50,700; Big Data Analytics and Logistics & Supply Chain cost ₹53,100.
  • Sikkim Manipal University (SMU) — ₹1,10,000 total. A dual-specialisation MBA — two specialisations in one degree — at roughly double the Mizoram fee but still far below most private universities.
  • Jamia Hamdard — ₹23,750 per semester. Charged semester-wise, which spreads the cost: you pay per semester instead of the full amount at once. The per-semester model is easier on a monthly budget than a lump-sum fee.
  • UPES Online — from ₹1,75,000 total. The starting fee for its MBA specialisations, including Digital Business, Business Analytics, International Business, Finance, Marketing, HR and Operations.
  • Dr. D.Y. Patil Pune — ₹1,89,400 total. A well-known private university MBA.
  • NMIMS CDOE — ₹1,96,000 total. One of the most recognised names in online management education in India.
  • Amity Online — ₹1,99,000 total. A large private university with 31 online programmes and wide specialisation choice.

A recognised online MBA in India costs anywhere from about ₹51,000 to several lakhs — the right choice is the lowest number that gives you what you need.

What "cheapest" does not include

A headline fee is not the full cost. Before you compare, ask each university for the total:

  • Registration and exam fees. Some universities charge these separately, and they add up over 2 years.
  • Payment structure. Semester-wise payment is much easier on a tight budget than a full lump-sum payment, even if the total is similar.
  • Specialisation pricing. Mizoram's Big Data Analytics MBA costs ₹53,100 while its General MBA costs ₹50,700. At other universities the gap between specialisations can be large, so always confirm the fee for your specialisation.

The trap: MBAs cheaper than ₹50,000

If you find an "MBA" for ₹20,000 or ₹30,000 from a name you have never heard of, stop. A real 2-year MBA from a UGC-approved university has real costs — faculty, LMS, exams, evaluation — and no recognised university sells it for the price of a smartphone. What those offers usually are:

  • A diploma or certificate labelled as an MBA. If the institute is not a UGC-approved university, employers and government recruiters will not accept it as a degree.
  • A foreign or unaccredited programme with no standing in India.
  • An advance-fee trap: a tiny first payment, then demands for exam fees, certificate fees and "verification charges" until you have paid far more than a real MBA.

The floor for a genuine online MBA in India is roughly ₹50,000. Anything far below that is a signal to verify.

How to choose on a budget

  • If your budget is under ₹60,000: Mizoram University's MBA at ₹50,700–₹53,100 is the genuine option — a central university degree, UGC-approved, and the fee is the full total.
  • If your budget is around ₹1 lakh: SMU's dual-specialisation MBA at ₹1,10,000 gives you two specialisations and the Manipal name.
  • If your budget stretches to ₹1,75,000–₹2,00,000: compare UPES (from ₹1,75,000), DY Patil (₹1,89,400), NMIMS (₹1,96,000) and Amity (₹1,99,000) on specialisation choice and placement support — not on fee alone.
  • Whatever your budget: verify UGC-DEB approval and confirm the total fee in writing.

Frequently Asked Questions

Which is the cheapest online MBA in India?

Mizoram University offers the cheapest genuine online MBA from a UGC-approved university, at ₹50,700–₹53,100 in total depending on specialisation. It is a central university degree, so the low fee comes from public-university pricing. The next cheapest on the list is Sikkim Manipal University's dual-specialisation MBA at ₹1,10,000.

Is a cheap online MBA valid?

Yes, if the university is UGC-DEB approved — validity depends on approval, not on price. Mizoram University is a central university set up under the UGC, so its low-fee MBA is as valid as a much more expensive MBA from a private university. What makes a cheap MBA invalid is the lack of UGC approval, so always verify the university on the UGC-DEB list.

Can I do an MBA for under ₹1 lakh?

Yes. Mizoram University's online MBA costs ₹50,700–₹53,100 in total, well under ₹1 lakh for the full 2-year degree from a UGC-approved central university. Sikkim Manipal University's dual-specialisation MBA at ₹1,10,000 is just above ₹1 lakh. Be cautious of any other "MBA under ₹1 lakh" that does not come from a UGC-approved university.

Are there extra charges over the MBA fee?

Usually, yes — most universities charge registration and exam fees separately, a few thousand rupees a year, on top of the headline programme fee. Always ask for the complete total in writing before enrolling, and prefer semester-wise payment if a lump sum is difficult for your family.
