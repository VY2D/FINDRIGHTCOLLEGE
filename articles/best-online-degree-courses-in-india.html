Best Online Degree Courses in India — MBA, BBA, BCA, MCA, B.Com, BA

The best online degree course in India depends on your goal. For management careers, the MBA is the strongest postgraduate option, with verified fees from ₹50,700 at Mizoram University to ₹3,70,000 at Symbiosis. For Class 12 pass-outs, the BBA, BCA, B.Com, and BA are the bachelor's options, with fees starting at ₹1,20,200 for a BBA and ₹78,000 for a BCA.

You are probably asking this right after Class 12 or graduation, with ten relatives giving ten different answers. There is no single best online degree — only the best degree for your goal, your budget, and your interest. This guide compares the six main online degree courses honestly, with verified fees.

MBA — the management degree (2 years, after graduation)

The online MBA is the most searched online degree in India, and for good reason — it is the fastest route to management roles for graduates and working professionals.

  • Lowest verified fee: Mizoram University, ₹50,700–₹53,100 total.
  • Budget picks: Sikkim Manipal (SMU) MBA with Dual Specialisation, ₹1,10,000; Jamia Hamdard MBA, ₹23,750 per semester.
  • Mid-range picks: LPU, ₹1,61,600 with the Student Grant; Chandigarh University, ₹1,58,000 with the Early Bird Discount; Dr. D.Y. Patil, ₹1,89,400; NMIMS, ₹1,96,000; Amity, ₹1,99,000.
  • Premium picks: UPES, ₹1,75,000–₹2,20,000 by specialisation; MAHE Manipal MBA for Working Professionals, ₹2,80,000; Symbiosis, ₹3,70,000.
  • Best for: Graduates and working professionals who want management, marketing, finance, HR, or analytics roles.

BBA — the business bachelor's (3 years, after Class 12)

The online BBA is the starting point for a management career, and the most common route is BBA followed by an MBA.

  • Lowest verified fee: Dr. D.Y. Patil, ₹1,20,200 total.
  • Mid-range picks: MUJ, ₹1,35,000; NMIMS, ₹1,31,000–₹1,45,000; LPU, ₹1,22,400 with the Student Grant; VGU, ₹1,32,000.
  • Premium picks: Amity, ₹1,65,000; Symbiosis, ₹1,65,000.
  • Per-year option: JAIN, ₹65,000–₹89,667 per year depending on the elective.
  • Best for: Class 12 students who want a business career and plan to add an MBA later.

BCA — the computer applications bachelor's (3 years, after Class 12)

The online BCA is the degree for students who want IT and software careers without an engineering degree.

  • Lowest verified fee: Shoolini, ₹78,000 under the Opt-Out model; JAIN, ₹45,000 per year.
  • Mid-range picks: MUJ, ₹1,35,000; Amity, ₹1,50,000; Symbiosis, ₹1,57,500; LPU, ₹1,22,400 with the Student Grant.
  • Best for: Students who like coding, computers, and technology, and want software, support, or data roles. Many BCA graduates add an MCA afterwards.

MCA — the IT master's (2 years, after graduation)

The online MCA is the natural next step after a BCA or any graduation with maths or computer background, and it opens deeper software and data roles.

  • Lowest verified fee: Sikkim Manipal (SMU), ₹98,000 total.
  • Mid-range picks: MUJ, ₹1,58,000; Amity, ₹1,70,000; UPES, ₹1,70,000; LPU, ₹1,29,600 with the Student Grant.
  • Premium pick: MAHE Manipal, ₹2,20,000.
  • Best for: Graduates who want software development, data, cybersecurity, or IT management careers.

B.Com — the commerce bachelor's (3 years, after Class 12)

The online B.Com is the classic commerce degree, suited to accounting, finance, banking, and CA or CS preparation alongside.

  • Lowest verified fee: Jamia Hamdard, ₹9,000 per semester.
  • Mid-range picks: NMIMS, ₹94,000; Amity, ₹99,000; MUJ, ₹99,000.
  • Best for: Commerce students who want accounting, banking, or finance careers, or who are preparing for CA, CS, or government exams alongside the degree.

BA — the arts bachelor's (3 years, after Class 12)

The online BA is the flexible option — English, Economics, Political Science, Journalism, and more — suited to civil services preparation, teaching, media, and content careers.

  • Lowest verified fee: Shoolini BA in Journalism and Mass Communication, ₹60,000; Amity BA, ₹99,000.
  • Other picks: Sharda BA, ₹1,05,000; LPU BA, ₹98,400 with the Student Grant.
  • Best for: Students preparing for UPSC or state exams, or aiming for teaching, media, writing, or social-sector careers, who need a valid degree with maximum flexibility.

How to choose your online degree

First, fix your goal — job, higher studies, or exam preparation. Second, fix your budget and strike off everything above it. Third, check UGC entitlement on the UGC-DEB website for any university you shortlist. Fourth, compare the total fee, not the first-semester figure, and check whether grants or discounts are included in the price you see.

Frequently Asked Questions

Which is the best online degree in India?

There is no single best online degree. For management careers, the MBA is the strongest option; for IT careers, the BCA followed by an MCA; for business careers after Class 12, the BBA; for commerce, the B.Com; and for flexible exam preparation, the BA. Pick the degree that matches your career goal, not the one that is most popular.

What degrees can I do 100% online?

You can do a full bachelor's or master's degree 100% online from a UGC-entitled university — MBA, BBA, BCA, MCA, B.Com, BA, M.Com, MA, and MSc programmes are all available online from the 17 universities on FindRightCollege. Classes, assignments, and exams are conducted online, and the degree is valid on the same terms as a regular degree from a UGC-approved university.

Is an online degree valid for government jobs?

Yes, provided the university is UGC-entitled to offer online degrees. A UGC-approved online degree is valid for government jobs and exams on the same terms as a regular degree. Always verify the university on the UGC-DEB website before admission — that one check is what separates genuine degrees from fake ones.

Which online degree is cheapest in India?

Among verified fees, the cheapest full degrees are Mizoram University's MBA at ₹50,700–₹53,100, Shoolini's BCA at ₹78,000, Jamia Hamdard's B.Com at ₹9,000 per semester, and LPU's diplomas at ₹40,800. The cheapest option is only right if it matches your career goal.

Ask Your Senior

My honest advice: do not pick a degree because it is popular — pick it because it leads where you want to go. If you want management, start with a BBA and plan an MBA. If you want IT, start with a BCA and plan an MCA. If you want a government job, pick a BA or B.Com and start preparing for the exam alongside. The degree is only the ticket — your skills and your next step decide the journey.
