Online Degree Admission 2026 — How to Apply, Eligibility and Process

To apply for an online degree in 2026, choose a UGC-entitled university, check the eligibility for your programme, apply on the university's official admission portal, upload your documents, pay the fee, and complete verification. For an MBA you generally need a bachelor's degree with 50% marks; for BBA, BCA and other undergraduate degrees you generally need a 10+2 pass.

Admission season brings a flood of calls, messages and agents — and most of the confusion is avoidable. The process itself is straightforward. What you need is the correct order of steps, the correct documents, and the patience to verify before you pay.

Step 1: Choose the degree and the university

First, decide the degree: MBA after graduation, or BBA, BCA, B.Com or BA after 12th. Then shortlist two or three universities by budget and by what you want after the degree. On this site, online MBA totals run from ₹50,700 (Mizoram University) to ₹4,00,000 (the NMIMS Executive MBA), with most standard MBAs between about ₹1,10,000 and ₹2,20,000. Undergraduate degrees vary more: for example, JAIN Online's BCA costs ₹45,000 per year, Amity's BCA costs ₹1,50,000 in total, and Jamia Hamdard's BCA costs ₹16,000 per semester. Fix your budget before you fall in love with a brand.

Step 2: Check the eligibility

Eligibility is simple but not identical everywhere:

  • Online MBA: a bachelor's degree (10+2+3 or equivalent) from a recognised university, usually with a minimum of 50% marks. Some universities relax this for candidates with work experience, and final-year graduation students can also apply at several universities.
  • Online BBA, BCA, B.Com, BA: a 10+2 pass from a recognised board. Some BCA programmes prefer Mathematics, Computer Science or IT at the 10+2 level, and a few ask other students to complete a bridge course in the first semester.
  • Online MCA: a bachelor's degree, usually with 50% marks, preferably with Mathematics at 10+2 or graduation level.

Read the eligibility line for your exact programme before applying, and do not trust an agent's summary over the university's own page.

Step 3: Verify UGC entitlement before paying

An online degree from a UGC-approved university is treated at par with a regular degree — but only if the university is entitled by UGC-DEB to offer that programme online. Check the university's name on the official UGC-DEB list before you pay anything. This one check separates a real degree from a worthless certificate. If a university or an agent cannot show you this, stop.

Step 4: Apply on the official portal

Apply on the university's own admission portal. The usual process is: register with your name, email and phone number; fill the application form; upload your documents; pay the application or registration fee; and wait for verification. Some universities — Chandigarh University Online and NMIMS, as per their brochures — also require an Academic Bank of Credits (ABC) ID and a UGC-DEB ID on the application form, so create those before you start. Admissions usually open twice a year, so a missed session is a delay of months, not a lost year. There is no entrance exam for the programmes listed on this site.

Step 5: Documents you will need

Keep these ready, scanned clearly: your 10th and 12th marksheets, your graduation degree or provisional certificate and marksheets (for PG programmes), a government photo ID such as Aadhaar, a passport-size photograph, and your ABC ID where required. Blurry scans and missing documents are the most common reason applications get delayed.

Step 6: Pay the fee safely

Pay only on the university's official payment page. Never transfer the fee to a personal account, a counsellor's account or an agent. Compare the full programme fee, not the semester fee — for example, Chandigarh University Online's MBA costs ₹1,58,000 with Early Bird Discount and ₹2,10,668 without it, and Amity's MBA costs ₹1,99,000 on instalments but ₹1,83,080 as a discounted one-time payment. Get the full fee in writing, including registration and examination charges, and keep every receipt.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I apply for an online degree in 2026?

Choose a UGC-entitled university, check the eligibility for your programme, and apply on the university's official admission portal. Register, fill the form, upload your documents, pay the application fee, and complete verification. Keep your 10th and 12th marksheets, graduation documents where needed, a photo ID, a photograph and your ABC ID ready before you start.

What is the eligibility for an online degree?

For undergraduate degrees such as BBA, BCA, B.Com and BA, you generally need a 10+2 pass from a recognised board. For an MBA you generally need a bachelor's degree with 50% marks, and for an MCA a bachelor's degree with 50% marks, preferably with Mathematics. Some universities relax the marks requirement for candidates with work experience. Always check the specific programme's eligibility.

Is there an entrance exam for online degrees?

No, there is no entrance exam for the online degree programmes listed on this site. Admission is based on your previous qualification's marks and the university's eligibility criteria. Be cautious of anyone who asks you to pay for entrance coaching or a guaranteed seat — neither is needed for these programmes.

When does online degree admission start in 2026?

Admissions usually open twice a year, and the exact dates differ by university. Check the admission page of the university you have shortlisted, and apply early — early applicants often get the best fee offers, such as Chandigarh University's Early Bird Discount. Do not wait for an agent to tell you; the university's own portal is the correct source.

Ask Your Senior

Do those three things properly, apply on the official portal yourself, and never pay anyone who is not the university.
