How Are Online MBA Exams Conducted? Exam Pattern, Papers and Passing Explained

Online MBA exams are conducted online, semester by semester, through a mix of internal assessments — assignments, quizzes and projects — and a term-end examination at the end of each semester. The term-end exam is usually remotely proctored, which means you appear for it from home under online supervision, and you book your exam slot in advance.

Exams are the part of an online degree that worries students the most, usually because nobody explains the pattern before admission. This article explains the full picture — and where the honest answer is "it depends on the university", I will say so instead of inventing a rule.

The semester structure

An online MBA runs for 2 years, divided into 4 semesters. Each semester has its own set of subjects, and each subject is assessed separately. You do not face one giant exam at the end of two years. You face a smaller, manageable set of assessments every six months. That is good news: if one semester goes badly, you can recover in the next one instead of losing a year.

The two parts of every subject

Most online MBA subjects are assessed in two parts:

  • Internal assessment: assignments, case-study submissions, quizzes and sometimes a project or presentation. These are completed during the semester, from home, on the learning platform. They count towards your final result, so ignoring them is the fastest way to fail a subject.
  • Term-end examination: a written exam at the end of the semester, conducted online. It usually covers the full subject, and it carries the larger share of the marks.

The exact weight of each part differs between universities, and it can differ between subjects in the same university. Do not trust a single percentage quoted on a random website. The correct source is the university's own student handbook or examination rules, which you receive after enrolment.

How the online exam actually happens

The term-end exam is usually a remotely proctored online exam. You log in from your home on the university's exam platform, your identity is verified, and the session is supervised through your webcam and screen. You need a stable internet connection, a working webcam and a quiet room. Universities generally let you book your exam slot in advance, choosing from available dates and times. The official brochures and university pages notes, for Manipal's programmes, that a student can give up to 3 examinations in one day, with slots allotted on a first-come, first-served basis — so booking early matters if you want your preferred schedule.

Passing, failing and improvement

Each university sets its own passing criteria — the minimum marks, the rules for passing internal assessment and term-end exam separately or together, and the grading scale. This article does not print a single passing percentage, because there is no single one across universities. If you fail a subject, universities generally allow a re-appear: you can sit for the subject again in a later exam cycle. The same applies to improvement, sometimes called betterment: if you want to improve your marks in a subject you already passed, most universities have a process for it, with their own rules and fees. Ask the university for its re-appear and improvement rules before you need them, not after a bad result.

How to prepare so you actually pass

Online exams are not easier than offline ones — they are only more flexible. The students who pass comfortably do four things: they submit every assignment on time, because internal marks are the easiest marks in the degree; they watch the recorded lectures instead of collecting them; they revise from the university's own study material, which is what the exam is set from; and they take at least one mock or practice test on the exam platform so the real exam holds no technical surprises. Do these four, and the exam pattern stops being scary.

Frequently Asked Questions

How are online MBA exams conducted?

Online MBA exams are conducted semester by semester, online. Each subject has internal assessment — assignments, quizzes and projects completed during the semester — and a term-end examination at the end of the semester, usually remotely proctored from home. You book your exam slot in advance on the university's exam platform.

Are online MBA exams easy?

No, they are not easy — they are flexible. The syllabus is the same management syllabus as a regular MBA, and the term-end exam is supervised. Students who submit assignments on time and revise from the university's study material generally pass comfortably. Students who ignore assignments and try to study everything in the last week struggle, online or offline.

Will I pass my online degree?

That depends on your own preparation, and no one can promise it for you. The structure helps you: semester-wise exams, internal assessment marks and re-appear options mean one bad paper does not end your degree. Submit assignments on time, study consistently through the semester, and use the re-appear option if a subject goes badly.

Can I give the exam from home?

Yes. Online MBA term-end exams are normally taken from home on the university's exam platform, under remote proctoring. You need a stable internet connection, a working webcam and a quiet room. Slot booking is usually on a first-come, first-served basis, so book your preferred date and time early.

Ask Your Senior

Do not fear the exam — respect it. The pattern is simple: assignments during the semester, one exam per subject at the end of it, and a second chance if something goes wrong. The students who fail online degrees are rarely the ones who found the exam hard; they are the ones who never submitted the assignments.
