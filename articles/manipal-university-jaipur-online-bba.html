Manipal University Jaipur (MUJ) Online BBA — Course, Fees and Details

Manipal University Jaipur (MUJ) offers its online BBA for ₹1,35,000 in total (₹22,500 per semester over 6 semesters). It is a 3-year undergraduate business degree, done fully online after Class 12, with seven specializations from marketing and finance to data analytics and digital marketing. The degree is valid only if the university holds UGC approval for online programmes.

You finished Class 12, you want to study business, and the Manipal name keeps coming up. Before your family pays ₹1,35,000, you deserve to know what the BBA teaches and which specialization fits you.

MUJ Online BBA at a Glance

  • University: Manipal University Jaipur (MUJ), Jaipur
  • Degree: Online BBA (Bachelor of Business Administration)
  • Duration: 3 years, 6 semesters
  • Total fee: ₹1,35,000 (₹22,500 per semester)
  • Eligibility: 10+2 pass from a recognised board is the standard requirement for the online BBA programmes listed on FindRightCollege. Confirm MUJ's exact criteria with the counsellor before you apply — eligibility is never guessed here.
  • Mode: Fully online — recorded lectures, live sessions, online exams and digital study material

MUJ is NAAC A+ accredited, and its online degrees started in 2021 with UGC approval. That history matters more than any advertisement. A degree's value comes from the university behind it, not from the website selling it.

What You Study in MUJ Online BBA

MUJ lists seven specializations for its online BBA: Human Resource Management, Marketing, Finance & Accounting, Entrepreneurship Management & Family Business, Data Analytics, Retail and E-Commerce, and Digital Marketing.

Across the six semesters, a BBA typically covers management principles, marketing, finance, accounting, human resources, business law, economics and organisational behaviour, with projects and case work in the later semesters. The specialization adds focused subjects in your chosen area.

Choose the specialization by what you enjoy. Marketing suits students who like people and ideas. Finance suits those who like numbers. Data Analytics suits those who like data. Entrepreneurship suits students from a family business. Picking one because it sounds impressive is how students end up bored for three years.

MUJ Online BBA Fees — and How They Compare

The MUJ online BBA fee is ₹1,35,000 in total, paid as ₹22,500 per semester. There is no separate headline fee and hidden total here — the per-semester figure and the total agree.

For comparison, here are other online BBA fees that pass the same check on FindRightCollege:

  • Mizoram University BBA: ₹55,700 total
  • Dr. D.Y. Patil Pune BBA: ₹1,20,200 total
  • NMIMS CDOE BBA: ₹1,31,000–₹1,45,000 total
  • JAIN Online BBA: ₹65,000 per year for HRM, Finance, Marketing and Engineering & Project Management, and ₹89,667 per year for Digital Marketing and Data Science & Analytics, plus a ₹2,500 one-time registration fee and a ₹3,000 yearly examination fee
  • Amity University Online BBA: ₹1,65,000 total (₹27,500 per semester)
  • UPES Online BBA: ₹1,50,000 total
  • Jamia Hamdard BBA: ₹14,000 per semester

MUJ sits in the middle of this range. It is not the cheapest option — Mizoram costs far less — and it is not the most expensive. If budget is your main worry, say so honestly before choosing. A cheaper degree from a UGC-approved university plus real skills beats an expensive degree with no skills every single time.

How Admission Works

Admission to an online BBA follows the same five steps across the universities listed here: choose your university, course and specialization; check the eligibility and keep your documents ready; fill the application form; upload your documents; and pay the fee on the official payment portal. Your 10th and 12th mark sheets and certificates, a government photo ID, recent photographs and your signature are the usual documents.

Pay only on the official portal, and keep every receipt. If anyone asks you to pay a "blocking amount" to a personal account, stop and ask us first.

Is an Online BBA from MUJ Valid?

An online degree is treated at par with a regular degree only when the university is UGC-approved to offer that programme online. That is the condition, every time, for every university. Before paying, check the university's UGC-DEB entitlement for the BBA programme, and verify it on the UGC-DEB portal yourself. A degree from a university that is not entitled for online delivery is not a bargain at any price.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the total fee of MUJ online BBA?

The total fee is ₹1,35,000, paid as ₹22,500 per semester over 6 semesters. This is the fee for Manipal University Jaipur (MUJ). It is the same total as MUJ's online BCA, and it sits in the middle of the verified online BBA fees on FindRightCollege, which run from ₹55,700 to ₹1,65,000.

What is the duration of the online BBA from MUJ?

It is a 3-year degree spread over 6 semesters. Online BBA programmes listed on FindRightCollege follow the same 3-year structure, so plan for three years of study alongside whatever else you are doing — a job, a family business, or preparation for something else.

Who can apply for MUJ online BBA?

Students who have passed 10+2 from a recognised board can apply for an online BBA. That is the standard eligibility across the online BBA programmes listed here. MUJ's exact criteria for your board and stream should be confirmed with the counsellor before you fill the form, because a wrong assumption at this stage costs a full semester.

Is MUJ online BBA good for jobs?

It can be, but the degree alone does not guarantee a job. An online BBA from a UGC-approved university makes you eligible for the same entry-level business roles as a regular BBA graduate, and hiring depends on your communication skill, your business understanding, practical experience and your interview.

Ask Your Senior

If you want to study business and you like the Manipal name, MUJ's online BBA at ₹1,35,000 is a fair, middle-of-the-road option. But do not choose it because of the name alone. Compare it with Mizoram at ₹55,700 and DY Patil at ₹1,20,200, ask yourself what the extra money is buying you, and pick the specialization you actually enjoy. A BBA works when you use the three years to learn how business really works — not when you only collect the degree.
