Chandigarh University Online MBA Fees — Full Fee Structure Explained

Chandigarh University's online MBA costs ₹1,80,400 with the Early Bird Discount (EBD), or ₹2,20,000 without it — a difference of ₹39,600. The fee is charged semester-wise: ₹45,100 per semester with the EBD, or ₹55,000 without it. The MBA is UGC-entitled, so it holds the same validity as a regular MBA.

You found two different fees for the same MBA and now you do not know which one is real. Both are real — and that is exactly the confusion this guide clears up. CU is one of the few universities that publishes both figures openly, but most websites quote only the lower one, so students budget ₹1,80,400 and are surprised later. Read this before you pay, and you will know exactly what you owe and when.

CU Online MBA — the full fee structure

  • With Early Bird Discount (EBD): ₹1,80,400 total (₹45,100 per semester × 4 semesters).
  • Without EBD: ₹2,20,000 total (₹55,000 per semester × 4 semesters).
  • Your saving with EBD: ₹39,600.
  • Duration: 2 years (4 semesters).
  • Prospectus fee: A small prospectus fee applies — confirm the current amount from CU when you apply, as it is separate from the course fee.
  • Degree validity: CU's online MBA is UGC-entitled, so it is valid for jobs, higher studies, and government exams on the same terms as a regular MBA from a UGC-approved university.

What is the Early Bird Discount — and will you get it?

The Early Bird Discount is a limited-period reduction CU offers to students who apply and enrol early in an admission cycle. It is not a permanent price cut, and it is not guaranteed — it depends on when you apply and whether the offer is still open. Before you budget ₹1,80,400, ask CU in writing whether the EBD applies to your admission cycle and what the last date is. If you miss the window, your fee is ₹2,20,000, and you should plan for that figure rather than hoping for the discount.

Specialisations you can choose

CU's online MBA offers a wide range of specialisations, including Marketing, Human Resource Management, Finance, Business Analytics, International Business, Entrepreneurship, Information Technology, Banking and Insurance, Logistics and Supply Chain Management, Hospital Management, Event Management, and Travel and Tourism Management. That breadth is CU's real strength — if you know the industry you want to work in, there is probably a specialisation for it. Pick the one that matches the job you want, not the one with the most fashionable name.

CU MBA fee vs other online MBAs

  • Chandigarh University Online MBA: ₹1,80,400 with EBD / ₹2,20,000 without EBD.
  • LPU Online MBA: ₹1,61,600 total with the current 20% Student Grant (actual fee ₹2,00,000) — a similar discount-based structure, so compare the with-grant and without-grant figures the same way.
  • Dr. D.Y. Patil Online MBA: ₹1,89,400 total, with no discount window to chase.
  • NMIMS Online MBA: ₹1,96,000 total, plus a ₹1,200 processing fee.
  • Amity Online MBA: ₹1,99,000 total.
  • UPES Online MBA: ₹1,75,000–₹2,20,000 depending on the specialisation.
  • Sikkim Manipal (SMU) MBA with Dual Specialisation: ₹1,10,000 total.
  • Mizoram University Online MBA: ₹50,700–₹53,100 total.
  • Symbiosis Online MBA: ₹3,70,000 total — the most expensive on this list.

With the EBD, CU is one of the better-value options among the big-brand MBAs. Without it, CU costs more than Amity, NMIMS, and D.Y. Patil — so the discount is not a small detail, it is the whole decision.

What to check before you pay

First, get the EBD in writing with its last date. Second, ask whether the semester fee includes the examination fee or whether exams are charged separately. Third, confirm the refund policy if you withdraw after admission. Fourth, check the total for your specialisation — some universities charge different fees for different MBA specialisations, so confirm yours specifically. Fifth, keep a copy of every fee receipt and confirmation email.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the fee of Chandigarh University's online MBA?

The online MBA costs ₹1,80,400 with the Early Bird Discount, or ₹2,20,000 without it. The semester fee is ₹45,100 with the EBD and ₹55,000 without it. Always confirm whether the EBD still applies to your admission cycle before you budget, because the difference is ₹39,600.

What is the Early Bird Discount in CU's online MBA?

The Early Bird Discount is a limited-period offer for students who apply and enrol early in an admission cycle. It reduces the total MBA fee from ₹2,20,000 to ₹1,80,400. It is not permanent and not guaranteed, so ask CU in writing whether it applies to your application and what the last date is.

Is the CU online MBA valid for jobs?

Yes. Chandigarh University's online MBA is UGC-entitled, so it is valid for private-sector jobs, higher studies, and government exams on the same terms as a regular MBA from a UGC-approved university. The degree's validity comes from UGC approval, not from whether you studied online or on campus.

Which CU online MBA specialisation is best?

It depends on your career goal. Finance and Business Analytics suit students who like numbers and data, Marketing suits students who like brands and communication, and HR suits students who like working with people. Logistics, Hospital Management, and Event Management suit students targeting those specific industries. Choose the specialisation that matches the job you want after the MBA.
