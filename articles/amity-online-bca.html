Amity University Online BCA — Course, Fees and Details

Amity University Online offers its online BCA for ₹1,50,000 in total (₹25,000 per semester over 6 semesters). It is a 3-year undergraduate degree in computer applications, done fully online after Class 12, with industry-linked variants in data analytics, cloud and software engineering. The degree is valid only if the university holds UGC approval for online programmes.

You have just passed Class 12 and your phone is full of Amity advertisements. The campus photos look impressive, the promises sound big, and the fee is not small. Before your family pays ₹1,50,000, you deserve the facts.

Amity Online BCA at a Glance

  • University: Amity University Online, Noida
  • Degree: Online BCA (Bachelor of Computer Applications)
  • Duration: 3 years, 6 semesters
  • Total fee: ₹1,50,000 (₹25,000 per semester)
  • Eligibility: 10+2 pass from a recognised board is the standard requirement for the online BCA programmes listed on FindRightCollege. Confirm Amity's exact criteria with your college senior before you apply — eligibility is never guessed here.
  • Mode: Fully online — recorded lectures, live sessions, online exams and digital study material

Amity Online lists 31 online programmes, one of the widest ranges on this site. A wide range is useful, but it is not a reason to choose a university. Choose on fee, subjects and what you will actually learn.

What You Study in Amity Online BCA

The regular Amity online BCA covers the standard computer applications subjects — programming, data structures, database management, operating systems, networking and web development — with projects in the later semesters.

Amity also offers industry-linked BCA variants with TCS iON and HCLTech, including Data Analytics, Cloud & Security, Data Engineering and Software Engineering. These variants carry the company's name and add industry-flavoured subjects and certifications to the degree. They cost more than the regular BCA, and the exact fee for your variant should be confirmed before admission, because variant fees change and should never be assumed from the regular BCA fee.

Pick a variant for its subjects, not its logo — recruiters still test what you can do.

Amity Online BCA Fees — and How They Compare

The regular Amity online BCA fee is ₹1,50,000 in total, paid as ₹25,000 per semester. Amity also publishes a discounted one-time fee option, so ask your college senior what the one-time payment figure is on the day you apply, and compare it with the semester-wise total before deciding.

For comparison, here are other online BCA fees that pass the same check on FindRightCollege:

  • Shoolini University BCA: ₹78,000 total
  • Mizoram University BCA: ₹64,700 total
  • MUJ (Manipal University Jaipur) BCA: ₹1,35,000 total (₹22,500 per semester)
  • JAIN Online BCA: ₹45,000 per year (₹1,35,000 total), plus a ₹2,500 one-time registration fee and a ₹3,000 yearly examination fee
  • UPES Online BCA: ₹1,50,000 total
  • Jamia Hamdard BCA: ₹16,000 per semester

Amity is at the higher end of this list, level with UPES and above MUJ and JAIN. The question to ask is simple: what are you getting for the extra money? If the answer is a variant with subjects you genuinely want, it may be worth it. If the answer is only the brand name, think twice.

How Admission Works

Admission follows the standard online-degree steps: choose your university, course and specialization; check the eligibility and keep your documents ready; fill the application form; upload your documents; and pay the fee on the official payment portal. Your 10th and 12th mark sheets and certificates, a government photo ID, recent photographs and your signature are the usual documents.

Amity's admission team may call you after you enquire. That is normal. What is not normal is pressure — "seat is filling, pay today" is a sales line, not a deadline. A real admission window gives you time to compare and decide.

Is an Online BCA from Amity Valid?

An online degree is treated at par with a regular degree only when the university is UGC-approved to offer that programme online. That is the condition, every time, for every university. Before paying, check Amity's UGC-DEB entitlement for the BCA programme on the UGC-DEB portal yourself. Validity is a fact you verify, not a promise you accept on a phone call.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the total fee of Amity University online BCA?

The regular online BCA costs ₹1,50,000 in total, paid as ₹25,000 per semester over 6 semesters. Industry-linked variants with TCS iON and HCLTech cost more, and their exact fee should be confirmed before admission. A discounted one-time payment option is also published, so compare both payment routes before you pay.

What is the duration of the online BCA from Amity?

It is a 3-year degree spread over 6 semesters. All online BCA programmes listed on FindRightCollege follow this 3-year structure. If you are working or preparing for something else alongside, plan your weeks around the semester schedule rather than trying to finish everything at the last minute.

Who can apply for Amity online BCA?

Students who have passed 10+2 from a recognised board can apply for an online BCA. That is the standard eligibility across the programmes listed here. Amity's exact criteria for your board, stream and subjects should be confirmed with your college senior before you fill the form.

Is Amity online BCA good for jobs?

It can be, but no degree guarantees a job. An online BCA from a UGC-approved university makes you eligible for the same entry-level IT roles as a regular BCA graduate, and hiring depends on your programming skill, your projects and your interview.

Ask Your Senior

Amity's online BCA is a solid, well-known option, but it is also one of the pricier BCAs on this list. Before you pay ₹1,50,000, compare it honestly with MUJ at ₹1,35,000 and Shoolini at ₹78,000, and ask what the difference is buying you. If you want the TCS iON or HCLTech variant for its subjects, go for it with open eyes. If you only want a good BCA degree, the cheaper UGC-approved options will take you to the same interviews.
