Symbiosis Online MBA Fees — Full Fee Structure Explained

Symbiosis Online's MBA costs ₹3,70,000 in total, payable as ₹1,85,000 per year over 2 years — the most expensive online MBA among the 17 universities on FindRightCollege. A work-experience scholarship brings it down to ₹3,15,000 for eligible students. The dual-specialisation MBA is UGC-entitled.

You are looking at Symbiosis because the name carries serious weight in Indian management education — and the fee reflects that. ₹3,70,000 is more than seven times the cost of Mizoram University's online MBA, so the question is not whether Symbiosis is good, but whether it is worth that much for you. This guide lays out the full fee structure, the scholarship, and the honest comparison, so you can decide with open eyes.

Symbiosis Online MBA — the full fee structure

  • Total fee: ₹3,70,000 (₹1,85,000 per year × 2 years).
  • With the work-experience scholarship: ₹3,15,000 total — a saving of ₹55,000 for eligible students with relevant work experience.
  • Duration: 2 years (4 semesters).
  • Format: Dual specialisation — you pick two specialisation areas instead of one.
  • Eligibility: Graduation in any discipline. Confirm the exact eligibility and the scholarship criteria from Symbiosis before applying.
  • Degree validity: Symbiosis International (Deemed University) is UGC-entitled, so the online MBA is valid for jobs, higher studies, and government exams on the same terms as a regular MBA from a UGC-approved university.

Dual specialisation combinations

The Symbiosis online MBA is built around dual specialisations — pairs like Marketing and Finance, Marketing and HR, Finance and Business Analytics, HR and Operations, and Finance and Hospital and Healthcare Management. A dual specialisation gives you two skill areas on one degree, which can help if you are targeting roles that cross functions, such as a marketing role that needs analytics. But be honest with yourself: two specialisations only help if you study both properly. A single specialisation done well beats two done halfway.

What the ₹3,70,000 buys — and what it does not

You are paying for the Symbiosis brand, the dual-specialisation format, and the university's long standing in management education. What the fee does not buy is a guaranteed job or a guaranteed salary — no honest university promises that, and Symbiosis is no exception. Before paying, confirm in writing whether the fee includes examination charges, whether the work-experience scholarship applies to your profile, and what the refund policy is. Also ask about the total for your specific specialisation pair, because fees can vary by combination.

Symbiosis MBA fee vs other online MBAs

  • Symbiosis Online MBA: ₹3,70,000 total (₹3,15,000 with the work-experience scholarship).
  • MAHE Manipal MBA for Working Professionals: ₹2,80,000 total.
  • UPES Online MBA: ₹1,75,000–₹2,20,000 depending on the specialisation.
  • Amity Online MBA: ₹1,99,000 total.
  • NMIMS Online MBA: ₹1,96,000 total, plus a ₹1,200 processing fee.
  • Dr. D.Y. Patil Online MBA: ₹1,89,400 total.
  • LPU Online MBA: ₹1,61,600 total with the current 20% Student Grant (actual fee ₹2,00,000).
  • Chandigarh University Online MBA: ₹1,58,000 with the Early Bird Discount / ₹2,10,668 without it.
  • Sikkim Manipal (SMU) MBA with Dual Specialisation: ₹1,10,000 total.
  • Mizoram University Online MBA: ₹50,700–₹53,100 total.

Symbiosis costs roughly double the Amity or NMIMS MBA and more than triple the SMU MBA. That gap only makes sense if the Symbiosis brand and the dual specialisation genuinely change your job prospects — for most students, they do not change them by ₹1,70,000 worth.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the total fee of the Symbiosis online MBA?

The total fee is ₹3,70,000, payable as ₹1,85,000 per year over 2 years. Students with eligible work experience can get the fee reduced to ₹3,15,000 through the work-experience scholarship. Always confirm the final fee and the scholarship criteria on the official website before admission.

Is there a scholarship for the Symbiosis online MBA?

Yes. Symbiosis offers a work-experience scholarship that brings the total fee down from ₹3,70,000 to ₹3,15,000 — a saving of ₹55,000. The scholarship depends on your work experience, so confirm the exact eligibility criteria from Symbiosis before counting on it.

Is the Symbiosis online MBA valid?

Yes. Symbiosis International (Deemed University) is UGC-entitled, so its online MBA is valid for private-sector jobs, higher studies, and government exams on the same terms as a regular MBA from a UGC-approved university. Verify the university's status on the UGC-DEB website yourself before paying.

Is the Symbiosis online MBA worth ₹3,70,000?

It depends on your situation. If the Symbiosis brand and a dual specialisation genuinely matter for the specific job or industry you are targeting, and your family can afford the fee comfortably, it can be worth it. If you simply need a valid MBA degree, Amity at ₹1,99,000, NMIMS at ₹1,96,000, or SMU at ₹1,10,000 give you the same validity for far less money.

Ask Your Senior

My honest take: Symbiosis is a great name, but ₹3,70,000 is a lot of money for an online MBA, and a degree's brand stops mattering the moment the interviewer starts asking what you can do. If you are a working professional and the work-experience scholarship brings it to ₹3,15,000, and the Symbiosis name opens a specific door for you, go for it. For most students, though, I would put the ₹1,70,000 you save by choosing Amity or NMIMS into skills, certifications, and internships — that investment will show up in your salary far more reliably than a brand name on a degree.
