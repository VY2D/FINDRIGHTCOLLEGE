How to Do an Online MBA in India — Step-by-Step Guide

To do an online MBA in India, you need a bachelor's degree, you must choose a UGC-entitled university, apply on its official admission portal, submit your documents, pay the fee, and complete the 2-year programme of 4 semesters through online classes, assignments and semester exams. There is no entrance exam for the programmes listed on this site.

That sounds simple, and the process itself is simple. The mistakes happen before the form: picking the wrong university, paying the wrong fee, or trusting an agent instead of the official process. Follow the steps below in order and you will avoid all three.

Step 1: Check your eligibility

For an online MBA you generally need a bachelor's degree (10+2+3 or equivalent) from a recognised university, usually with a minimum of 50% marks. Some programmes relax this: candidates with 2 years of work experience can often apply with 45%, and Amity's Hospital and Healthcare MBA accepts 40%, with a test option below that. Final-year graduation students can also apply at several universities. Do not assume — read the eligibility line for your chosen programme before you apply, because eligibility differs slightly between universities.

Step 2: Shortlist by budget and goal, not by advertisement

Fix your total budget first. On this site, total online MBA fees run from ₹50,700 (Mizoram University) to ₹4,00,000 (the NMIMS Executive MBA for senior professionals). Most standard online MBAs fall between about ₹1,10,000 and ₹2,20,000 in total. Shortlist two or three universities in your budget, then match the specialisation to your next job — Marketing, Finance, HR, Business Analytics, Operations or a niche such as Oil and Gas at UPES. Only then compare brand names. A university you cannot afford is not a shortlist.

Step 3: Verify the university is UGC-entitled

This is the step most students skip, and it is the most important one. An online degree from a UGC-approved university is treated at par with a regular degree — but only if the university is entitled by UGC-DEB to offer that programme online. Check the university's name on the official UGC-DEB list before you pay anything. If a university or an agent cannot show you this, stop.

Step 4: Apply on the official portal

Apply on the university's own admission portal, not through an agent's link. The usual process is: register, fill the application form, upload your documents, pay the application or registration fee, and wait for verification. Some universities — Chandigarh University Online and NMIMS, as per their brochures — also require an Academic Bank of Credits (ABC) ID and a UGC-DEB ID on the application form, so create those in advance. Admissions usually open twice a year, so if you miss one session, the next one is only months away.

Step 5: Keep your documents ready

Keep these ready before you start the form: your 10th and 12th marksheets, your graduation degree or provisional certificate and marksheets, a government photo ID, a passport-size photograph, and your ABC ID where required. Having them scanned and ready makes the whole process take an hour instead of a week.

Step 6: Pay carefully, and keep proof

Pay only on the university's official payment page or portal. Never transfer the fee to a personal account, an individual's account or an agent. Compare the total programme fee, not just the semester fee — for example, Chandigarh University Online costs ₹1,80,400 with Early Bird Discount and ₹2,20,000 without it, and Amity's MBA costs ₹1,99,000 on instalments but ₹1,83,080 as a discounted one-time payment. Ask for the full fee in writing, including registration and examination charges, and keep every receipt.

Step 7: Start studying from week one

Once enrolled, you get access to the learning platform: recorded lectures, live sessions, study material and assignment deadlines. The MBA runs for 2 years across 4 semesters, with internal assessments and a term-end exam each semester. Protect a few study hours every week from the start. Students who fall behind in the first semester are the ones who struggle most later.

Frequently Asked Questions

How can I do an online MBA from home?

You can do the entire MBA from home: apply online, study through recorded lectures and live sessions, submit assignments online and appear for semester exams online. You need a bachelor's degree, a UGC-entitled university and a stable internet connection. No relocation and no campus attendance are required for the programmes listed on this site.

Is there an entrance exam for an online MBA?

No, there is no entrance exam for the online MBA programmes listed on this site. Admission is based on your graduation marks and the university's eligibility criteria. Be cautious of anyone who asks you to pay for entrance coaching or a guaranteed seat — neither is needed for these programmes.

What is the minimum eligibility for an online MBA?

Generally, a bachelor's degree in any discipline from a recognised university, usually with a minimum of 50% marks. Some universities relax this to 45% for candidates with 2 years of work experience, and Amity's Hospital and Healthcare MBA accepts 40%, with a test option below that. Final-year students can also apply at several universities. Always check the specific programme's eligibility before applying.

How long does an online MBA take?

An online MBA takes 2 years, divided into 4 semesters. Each semester has internal assessments or assignments and a term-end examination. You study alongside your job or other commitments, and you finish with the same degree value as a regular MBA from a UGC-entitled university.
