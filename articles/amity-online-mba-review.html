Amity University Online MBA Review — Is It Good?

Amity University Online's MBA is a solid, flexible option for learners who want a big, established brand, a wide choice of specialisations and instalment payments. It is not the cheapest MBA, and its huge catalogue can confuse you, but the degree is genuine: from a UGC-approved university, it is treated at par with a regular degree.

That is the short answer. This review is not a sales pitch, and I have not invented any rating or ranking for it. Everything below comes from the official brochures and university pages, the brochure fee table and the university's own published details.

What Amity University Online actually offers

Amity Online lists 31 online programmes, which is the widest range on this site. Its MBA is a 2-year programme (4 semesters), and it covers the standard specialisations — Marketing, Finance, HR, Operations and Business Analytics among them — plus niche options such as a Hospital and Healthcare MBA. The university notes industry-linked programmes with HCL and TCS-iON, and offers BCA and MBA variants built with these companies. Its programmes are also noted as accredited by WES in the US and Canada, which matters if you ever plan to study or work abroad.

The real fees

The brochure fee table in the official brochures and university pages is unusually transparent, and it teaches a useful lesson: how you pay changes what you pay.

  • MBA — ₹1,99,000 total on semester instalments (₹49,750 per semester); a discounted one-time fee of ₹1,83,080 is also listed.
  • MBA in Hospital and Healthcare — offered on zero-cost EMI of ₹13,023 per month, as per the brochure.
  • BBA — ₹1,65,000 total (₹27,500 per semester).
  • BCA — ₹1,50,000 total (₹25,000 per semester); BCA variants with TCS-iON or HCLTech cost ₹2,25,000 total.
  • MCA — ₹1,70,000 total (₹42,500 per semester).
  • B.Com — ₹99,000 total (₹16,500 per semester).
  • M.Com — ₹1,20,000 total (₹30,000 per semester).
  • BA — ₹99,000 total (₹16,500 per semester).

The MBA's instalment total and its one-time fee differ by nearly ₹16,000. That is not a trick — it is a real choice. If you can pay at once, ask for the one-time figure in writing before you pay.

What is genuinely good about it

  • Brand recognition: Amity is a well-known name, and that helps your resume get read.
  • Choice: with 31 programmes, you can switch direction — from BBA to MBA, or from BCA to MCA — without changing universities.
  • Flexibility: semester-wise instalments, recorded learning and an EMI option for the healthcare MBA.
  • Industry-linked variants: the HCLTech and TCS-iON variants add an industry name to your degree, at ₹2,25,000 for the BCA versions.

What to watch out for

  • Price: at ₹1,99,000, the MBA costs more than Mizoram University (₹50,700–₹53,100), SMU (₹1,10,000) or JAIN Online (₹98,000–₹1,40,000 per year). Pay the premium only if the brand and the specialisation genuinely matter for your next job.
  • Choice overload: 31 programmes and many specialisations make it easy to pick the wrong one. Do not choose by the brochure's longest list.
  • Placement support, not placement: Amity offers career support, not a job guarantee. No university should promise you one.
  • Confirm the total: always get the full fee — including any registration and examination charges — in writing before paying.

Who should choose it, and who should not

Choose Amity's online MBA if you want a recognised brand, you need instalment payments, and your target job values the university's name. Look elsewhere if your budget is under ₹1,00,000 — Mizoram University or SMU will serve you better — or if you want the lowest possible fee for the same UGC-entitled degree. And whichever university you pick, first check its UGC-DEB entitlement, because only a UGC-approved university's online degree equals a regular degree.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Amity University Online MBA good?

Yes, it is a genuine and flexible MBA from a well-known university, with a wide choice of specialisations and instalment payments. Whether it is good for you depends on your budget and your goal: at ₹1,99,000 it costs more than several equally valid UGC-entitled options, so the brand and the specialisation need to be worth the difference to you.

What is the fee of the Amity online MBA?

The brochure lists ₹1,99,000 in total on semester instalments (₹49,750 per semester), and a discounted one-time fee of ₹1,83,080. The Hospital and Healthcare MBA is offered on zero-cost EMI of ₹13,023 per month. Always confirm the current total, including registration and examination charges, in writing before you pay.

Is Amity online MBA valid?

Yes, provided you enrol through the university's UGC-entitled online programmes. An online degree from a UGC-approved university is treated at par with a regular degree. Check the university's current UGC-DEB entitlement on the official UGC-DEB list before paying any fee.

Does Amity online MBA give placement?

Amity provides career support and industry-linked programmes, including industry-linked programmes with HCL and TCS-iON, but it does not guarantee a job — and no genuine university does. Placement outcomes depend on your specialisation, your prior experience and your own effort.
