Amity University Online MBA Fees — Full Fee Structure Explained

Amity University Online's general online MBA costs ₹1,99,000 in total, payable as ₹49,750 per semester over 2 years. A one-time discounted fee of ₹1,83,080 is also listed for students who pay upfront. The MBA is UGC-entitled, so it holds the same validity as a regular MBA from a UGC-approved university.

You are comparing MBA fees because ₹2 lakh is serious money for most families — and the worst way to spend it is on a fee you did not fully understand. Some universities quote a discounted fee and hide the real one; some add exam and registration charges later. This guide lays out Amity's MBA fee structure exactly as it appears in the official brochures and university pages and on FindRightCollege, so you know what you are paying for before you pay it.

Amity Online MBA — the full fee structure

  • General Online MBA: ₹1,99,000 total (₹49,750 per semester × 4 semesters).
  • One-time discounted fee: ₹1,83,080, listed in the official brochures and university pages for students who pay the full fee upfront — a saving of ₹15,920 against the semester-wise total.
  • Duration: 2 years (4 semesters).
  • Eligibility: Graduation in any discipline. The brochure lists a minimum of 50% marks for the general MBA, with relaxation for reserved categories.
  • Degree validity: Amity University Online is UGC-entitled, so the online MBA is valid for jobs, higher studies, and government exams on the same terms as a regular MBA from a UGC-approved university.

Specialised MBA variants and their fees

Amity offers MBA variants beyond the general programme, and each has its own fee:

  • Online MBA (ACCA) — International Finance: ₹2,99,000 total (₹74,750 per semester). This is for students who want an MBA alongside ACCA-aligned finance training.
  • Online MBA in Hospital and Healthcare Management (with Medvarsity): The brochure lists a zero-cost EMI of ₹13,023 per month for Indian students, but the total fee is not stated clearly in the brochure, so I am not quoting a total here — confirm it from Amity before admission.
  • Other specialisations in the general MBA: Business Analytics, Data Science, Digital Entrepreneurship, Digital Marketing Management, Finance and Accounting, Global Finance Market, HR, HR Analytics, IT Management, Insurance, International Business, and more. The general ₹1,99,000 fee covers these specialisations.

What to check before you pay

First, confirm whether the ₹1,99,000 total includes the examination fee or whether exam and registration charges apply separately — ask Amity for the complete fee sheet in writing, not just the headline number. Second, ask whether the one-time discount of ₹1,83,080 is still available when you apply. Third, read the refund policy. Fourth, confirm the EMI terms in writing if you plan to pay in instalments — "zero-cost EMI" should mean no extra interest, and that should be on paper.

Amity MBA fee vs other online MBAs

  • Amity Online MBA: ₹1,99,000 total.
  • Manipal University Jaipur (MUJ) MBA: ₹1,80,000 total (₹45,000 per semester), plus a ₹500 application fee.
  • Dr. D.Y. Patil Online MBA: ₹1,89,400 total.
  • NMIMS Online MBA: ₹1,96,000 total, plus a ₹1,200 processing fee.
  • UPES Online MBA: ₹1,75,000–₹2,20,000 depending on the specialisation.
  • LPU Online MBA: ₹1,61,600 total with the current 20% Student Grant (actual fee ₹2,00,000).
  • Sikkim Manipal (SMU) MBA with Dual Specialisation: ₹1,10,000 total.
  • Mizoram University Online MBA: ₹50,700–₹53,100 total.
  • Symbiosis Online MBA: ₹3,70,000 total — the most expensive on this list.

Amity sits in the upper-middle range. You are paying for a large, well-known brand and a wide specialisation list — whether that is worth it depends on whether you will use the brand and the specialisation, not on the fee alone.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the total fee of the Amity online MBA?

The general online MBA costs ₹1,99,000 in total, payable as ₹49,750 per semester over 2 years. A one-time discounted fee of ₹1,83,080 is listed in the official brochures and university pages for upfront payment. Always confirm the final fee on the official website before admission, because fees can change between admission cycles.

Is there any extra fee apart from the ₹1,99,000?

Possibly, depending on your specialisation. Universities often charge examination, registration, or project fees on top of the headline course fee, so ask Amity in writing for the complete fee sheet before you pay. Also confirm whether the one-time discount is still available when you apply.

Is the Amity online MBA valid for jobs?

Yes. Amity University Online is UGC-entitled, so its online MBA is valid for private-sector jobs, higher studies, and government exams on the same terms as a regular MBA from a UGC-approved university. What the degree cannot do is guarantee a job — your specialisation, internships, and interview performance decide that.

Which Amity MBA specialisation is best?

It depends on your career goal. Finance and Business Analytics suit students who like numbers and data, Marketing and Digital Marketing suit students who like communication and brands, and HR suits students who like working with people. Pick the specialisation that matches the job you want, not the one that sounds most fashionable.

Ask Your Senior

₹1,99,000 is a fair fee for Amity's brand and specialisation range — but only if you actually use what you are paying for. If you just want any MBA degree, SMU at ₹1,10,000 or Mizoram at around ₹51,000 will give you the same valid degree for far less. Choose Amity when the brand name and the specific specialisation genuinely matter for the job you want. And whatever you choose, confirm every extra charge in writing before paying — the fee you see first is rarely the fee you finally pay.
