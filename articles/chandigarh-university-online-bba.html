What is verified about Chandigarh University Online

  • Online MBA fee: ₹1,58,000 with the Early Bird Discount (EBD), or ₹2,10,668 without it. The EBD saves ₹52,668, so always ask whether the discount still applies when you apply.
  • Online MBA specialisations: CU's online MBA offers a wide range, including Marketing, HRM, Finance, Business Analytics, International Business, Entrepreneurship, IT, Banking and Insurance, and Logistics and Supply Chain Management.
  • Fee transparency: CU publishes the EBD and non-EBD figures separately, which is genuinely helpful — many universities hide the real fee behind a single discounted number.
  • What is not verified: The online BBA's total fee and its specialisation list are not published on FindRightCollege, and the counsellor guide we work from does not carry a CU BBA fee table either. That is why no BBA fee appears in this article.

Why I will not quote a CU BBA fee

Questions to ask CU before you apply

Ask these five questions in writing before paying any fee: what is the total BBA fee for the full 3 years, and what is the semester-wise breakup? Which specialisations are offered in the online BBA? Are classes live, recorded, or both? How are exams conducted — online proctored, or at a centre? And what is the refund policy if you withdraw? A university that answers these clearly is a university you can trust with your money.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the fee of Chandigarh University's online BBA?

The fee is not listed on FindRightCollege, and the counsellor guide does not carry a CU BBA fee table, so I am not quoting a number here. CU's online MBA fee is verified — ₹1,58,000 with the Early Bird Discount or ₹2,10,668 without it — but the BBA fee must be confirmed directly from Chandigarh University before you plan your budget.

Is Chandigarh University's online BBA valid?

An online degree is valid when the university is UGC-entitled to offer online programmes, and CU is a recognised university offering UGC-entitled online degrees. Before admission, verify CU's name on the UGC-DEB website yourself — that five-minute check is the only confirmation that truly matters, for CU or for any university.

What should I compare CU's online BBA with?

Compare it with verified options: Dr. D.Y. Patil at ₹1,20,200, MUJ at ₹1,35,000, NMIMS at ₹1,31,000–₹1,45,000, and Amity and Symbiosis at ₹1,65,000. Once CU confirms its BBA fee, place it in this list and judge it on fee, specialisations, and learning format — not on brand name alone.

Can I do an MBA after CU's online BBA?

Yes. A BBA from a UGC-entitled university is a recognised bachelor's degree, so you are eligible for MBA admission — online or regular — after completing it. CU itself offers an online MBA, and the BBA-then-MBA route is one of the most common paths in management education.
