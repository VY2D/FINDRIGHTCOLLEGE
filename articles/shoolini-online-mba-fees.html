Shoolini University Online MBA Fees — Full Fee Structure Explained

Shoolini University offers its online MBA as the Power MBA, plus a Shoolini × KPMG in India MBA — but no fee figure is quoted in this article. The website and the counsellor guide list different totals across the discounted, regular, Pay After Placement, and Opt-Out models, so confirm the current fee directly from Shoolini.

You came here for a number, and I am telling you I do not have a reliable one — that is frustrating, but it is better than a wrong number. A wrong fee can wreck a family's budget planning, and Shoolini's own sources currently disagree with each other. This guide explains what is verified about Shoolini's online MBA, what the payment models mean, and which budget MBAs have confirmed fees you can compare with.

What is verified about Shoolini's online MBA

  • The programme: Shoolini's online MBA is called the Power MBA, and there is also a Shoolini × KPMG in India MBA variant.
  • Duration: 2 years (4 semesters).
  • Specialisations: The Power MBA offers a wide range, including Marketing, Finance, HR, Digital Marketing, Retail, Operations, Banking and Insurance, Logistics and Supply Chain, Tourism, Real Estate, Direct Selling, Data Science and Business Analytics, Agri Business, Biotechnology, and Pharma and Healthcare Management.
  • Degree validity: Shoolini University is UGC-approved, so its online MBA is valid for jobs, higher studies, and government exams on the same terms as a regular MBA from a UGC-approved university — provided you confirm its UGC-DEB entitlement for online programmes before admission.
  • What is not verified: The exact total fee. That is why no fee figure appears in this article.

Why the fee is not quoted here

Shoolini's fee structure uses more than one model. The counsellor guide shows a Pay After Placement model and an Opt-Out model with different totals, and the website shows a discounted fee against a regular fee — and the numbers across these sources do not match. On FindRightCollege, a fee is printed only when the plan, the counsellor guide, and the website agree. For Shoolini's MBA, they do not agree yet. Rather than pick one source and hope it is right, this article waits for the sources to be reconciled.

What Pay After Placement and Opt-Out mean

Two payment ideas appear in Shoolini's guide, and they are worth understanding even without the numbers. Under Pay After Placement, you pay a smaller amount upfront and the remaining fee after you are placed in a job. Under Opt-Out, you pay the full course fee upfront. Before choosing either, ask Shoolini in writing: what is the total under each model, what counts as "placement", and what happens if placement does not happen.

Shoolini fees that are verified

These Shoolini programme fees are confirmed across the counsellor guide and the website:

  • Online BCA: ₹78,000 under the Opt-Out model, or ₹80,550 under Pay After Placement.
  • Online MCA: ₹82,000 total (₹20,500 per semester).
  • BA in Journalism and Mass Communication: ₹60,000 total (₹10,000 per semester).

Verified budget MBA options to compare with

While you wait for Shoolini's confirmed MBA fee, these are the online MBA fees that are verified across our sources:

  • Mizoram University Online MBA: ₹50,700–₹53,100 total, depending on the specialisation.
  • Sikkim Manipal (SMU) MBA with Dual Specialisation: ₹1,10,000 total (₹27,500 per semester).
  • Jamia Hamdard Online MBA: ₹23,750 per semester, plus a ₹2,500 exam fee and ₹500 registration.
  • LPU Online MBA: ₹1,61,600 total with the current 20% Student Grant (actual fee ₹2,00,000).
  • Chandigarh University Online MBA: ₹1,58,000 with the Early Bird Discount / ₹2,10,668 without it.
  • Dr. D.Y. Patil Online MBA: ₹1,89,400 total.
  • Amity Online MBA: ₹1,99,000 total.
  • NMIMS Online MBA: ₹1,96,000 total, plus a ₹1,200 processing fee.

If Shoolini's confirmed fee lands near its budget positioning, it would compete with SMU and Mizoram. If it lands higher, compare it with Amity and NMIMS on the same terms.

Questions to ask Shoolini before you apply

Ask these in writing: what is the total Power MBA fee right now, and is the discounted fee still available? What is the total under Pay After Placement versus Opt-Out? What exactly counts as placement under the Pay After Placement model? Are examination fees included or extra? And what is the refund policy if you withdraw? Get the answers on paper before paying anything.
