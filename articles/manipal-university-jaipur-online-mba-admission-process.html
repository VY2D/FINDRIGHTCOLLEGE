Manipal University Jaipur Online MBA Admission Process — Step by Step

Manipal University Jaipur (MUJ) admits students to its online MBA directly on graduation marks, with no entrance exam. The process is: check your eligibility, choose one of the 13 specialisations, fill the online application, upload your documents, pay the fee, and complete verification. Confirm the current MBA fee with the university before paying.

You have picked Manipal University Jaipur for your online MBA and you want the admission process plainly — the steps and the documents, without a sales call in between. Here it is, including one fee warning you need to read before you pay.

A fee warning first

The MBA fee for Manipal University Jaipur needs a careful check. The counsellor brochure and the FindRightCollege website show different figures for the online MBA fee. Because the two sources disagree, this article does not state any figure as final. Do not pay based on a screenshot or a forwarded message — confirm the exact fee on MUJ's official admission page or with the university itself, and keep a written record of the figure you were quoted.

MUJ's other programme fees are consistent across sources: online MCA at ₹1,58,000, online BBA and BCA at ₹1,35,000 each, M.Com at ₹1,08,000 and B.Com at ₹99,000.

Step 1: Check that you are eligible

MUJ's online MBA is a postgraduate programme, so you need a bachelor's degree from a recognised university. Most programmes listed on FindRightCollege ask for 50% marks in graduation — confirm the exact requirement on the university's admission page before applying, because the requirement is set by the university.

There is no entrance exam. None of the online MBA brochures listed on FindRightCollege asks for a CAT, MAT or any other entrance-exam score, and MUJ is no exception. Admission is based on your qualifying-exam marks.

Step 2: Choose your specialisation

MUJ offers 13 MBA specialisations — one of the widest lists on FindRightCollege:

  • Digital Marketing, Marketing, Finance, Human Resource Management
  • Analytics and Data Science, BFSI, IT and FinTech
  • Operations Management, International Business, Information System Management
  • Project Management, Supply Chain Management, Retail Management

Do not choose by what sounds impressive. Choose by the work you want: Finance for number-driven roles, Marketing or Digital Marketing for brand and growth roles, HR for people-management roles, Analytics and Data Science for data-driven roles, and Operations or Supply Chain for logistics and process roles. If you are torn between two, remember that Sikkim Manipal University offers a dual-specialisation MBA — but at MUJ, you pick one and go deep.

Step 3: Fill the online application

Apply on Manipal University Jaipur's official online admission portal. You will fill in your personal details, education history and your chosen programme and specialisation. Keep these ready before you start:

  • Your graduation mark sheets and degree certificate (or provisional certificate if the final degree is still awaited)
  • Class 10 and Class 12 mark sheets
  • A government ID proof
  • A recent passport-size photograph

Fill in your own details and double-check your name, date of birth and email address. A wrong email means you will miss every update the university sends.

Step 4: Pay the fee

Confirm the exact MBA fee on MUJ's official page first — as noted above, the brochure and the website disagree, so do not rely on either figure alone. Once the university confirms your fee, pay only through the university's official payment link or portal. Never transfer the fee to a personal account, a counsellor's account or a random UPI ID.

The brochure mentions discounts of 10% on overall fees and 5% on annual payment. Ask the university which discount applies to your payment plan before you pay.

Step 5: Document verification and enrolment

After you apply and pay, the university verifies your documents. Once verification is complete, you receive your enrolment confirmation and login details for the online learning platform. Your classes, study material, assignments and exams all run through that platform.

The online MBA is a two-year programme spread across four semesters. Before the first class, log in once, check that your subjects and specialisation are correct, and confirm your exam schedule. If anything looks wrong, raise it with the university immediately.

A note on validity

Manipal University Jaipur is a UGC-approved university with NAAC A+ accreditation, and it has offered UGC-approved online degrees since 2021. That is the condition that matters: only a UGC-approved university's online degree is treated as equal to a regular degree. Before you pay any fee, verify the university's UGC-DEB approval yourself — it takes two minutes and it is the single most important check in this whole process.
