UPES Online MBA Fees — Full Fee Structure Explained

UPES Online's MBA costs between ₹1,75,000 and ₹2,20,000 in total, depending on the specialisation. The lowest-fee specialisations, including Digital Business, Business Analytics, Finance, Marketing, HR, and Operations, cost ₹1,75,000. Oil and Gas Management and Power Management cost ₹2,20,000. The MBA is UGC-entitled.

You probably found UPES because of its energy-sector reputation — it is the University of Petroleum and Energy Studies, after all. But many students see "₹1,75,000–₹2,20,000" and have no idea which end of that range applies to them. The difference is ₹45,000, which is real money. This guide breaks the fee down specialisation by specialisation, so you know your number before you apply.

UPES Online MBA — fee by specialisation

All fees below are exclusive of GST, as listed in the counsellor guide and on FindRightCollege. Confirm the GST amount and the final payable fee from UPES before admission.

  • ₹1,75,000 — Digital Business, Infrastructure Management, Business Analytics, International Business, Finance, Marketing, HR, and Operations Management. These are the general management specialisations, and they sit at the lower end of the range.
  • ₹1,95,000 — Logistics and Supply Chain Management (with an Industry Experiential Programme by Safexpress).
  • ₹2,20,000 — Oil and Gas Management and Power Management. These are UPES's signature energy-sector specialisations, and they carry the highest fee.
  • Duration: 2 years (4 semesters).
  • Eligibility: Graduation in any discipline.
  • Degree validity: UPES Online is UGC-entitled, so the online MBA is valid for jobs, higher studies, and government exams on the same terms as a regular MBA from a UGC-approved university.

What the fee does and does not include

The figures above exclude GST, so the amount you actually pay will be higher than the headline fee — ask UPES for the final amount including GST in writing. Also ask whether the examination fee is included or charged separately, and read the refund policy. UPES also lists scholarships in its guide — for example, academic scholarships linked to work experience and graduation marks — but the terms can change, so confirm what applies to your profile before counting on any reduction.

  • Online BCA: ₹1,50,000 total (excluding GST).

  • PGP programmes: ₹75,000–₹95,000 depending on the programme (excluding GST). The PGP is a shorter, skill-focused option for working professionals who want a career boost without a full 2-year MBA.

  • UPES Online MBA: ₹1,75,000–₹2,20,000 by specialisation.
  • Amity Online MBA: ₹1,99,000 total.
  • NMIMS Online MBA: ₹1,96,000 total, plus a ₹1,200 processing fee.
  • Dr. D.Y. Patil Online MBA: ₹1,89,400 total.
  • LPU Online MBA: ₹1,61,600 total with the current 20% Student Grant (actual fee ₹2,00,000).
  • Chandigarh University Online MBA: ₹1,58,000 with the Early Bird Discount / ₹2,10,668 without it.

  • Symbiosis Online MBA: ₹3,70,000 total.

If you want a general MBA, UPES at ₹1,75,000 is competitive with Amity and NMIMS. If you want the energy specialisations, UPES at ₹2,20,000 is the premium you pay for a niche that few other online MBAs offer.

Who should pick UPES's online MBA

  • Students targeting the energy, oil and gas, or power sectors, where UPES's name and industry links carry real weight.
  • Working professionals in logistics and supply chain, for the Safexpress-linked specialisation.
  • Students who want a general MBA at ₹1,75,000 with a recognised university name.
  • Students who can handle the GST on top — if the total including GST stretches your budget, a university that quotes an all-inclusive fee may be simpler to plan around.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the fee of the UPES online MBA?

The total fee is ₹1,75,000–₹2,20,000 depending on the specialisation, excluding GST. General specialisations like Finance, Marketing, HR, and Business Analytics cost ₹1,75,000, while Oil and Gas Management and Power Management cost ₹2,20,000. Always confirm the final fee including GST from UPES before admission.

Is the UPES online MBA valid?

Yes. UPES Online is UGC-entitled, so its online MBA is valid for private-sector jobs, higher studies, and government exams on the same terms as a regular MBA from a UGC-approved university. Verify the university's status on the UGC-DEB website yourself before paying.

Which UPES MBA specialisation is best?

It depends on your career goal. Oil and Gas Management and Power Management are best if you want to work in the energy sector, where UPES has its strongest reputation. Logistics and Supply Chain suits operations careers, and Finance, Marketing, HR, and Business Analytics suit general management roles. Do not pick an energy specialisation just because it sounds premium — pick it only if you want an energy career.

Does the UPES MBA fee include GST?

No. The fees listed in the counsellor guide and on FindRightCollege are exclusive of GST, so the final amount you pay will be higher. Ask UPES for the total payable amount including GST in writing before you plan your budget.
