Amity University Online BBA — Course, Fees and Details

Yes, Amity University Online offers a 3-year online BBA for a total fee of ₹1,65,000 (₹27,500 per semester), and it is a UGC-entitled online degree, which means it holds the same validity as a regular BBA from a UGC-approved university. You study fully online with live and recorded classes, in a General BBA covering the core business subjects.

You have just finished Class 12, and everyone around you has an opinion — one relative says BBA is useless without an MBA, another says only a regular college counts. Meanwhile, you are wondering whether you can study from home, save on hostel and travel costs, and still hold a degree that employers respect. That confusion is exactly what this guide clears up, with real numbers and no sales talk.

Amity Online BBA — the course at a glance

  • Duration: 3 years (6 semesters), fully online.
  • Total fee: ₹1,65,000, payable as ₹27,500 per semester. A one-time discounted fee of ₹1,51,800 is also listed in the counsellor guide for students who pay upfront.
  • Eligibility: Pass in 10+2 from a recognised board.
  • Degree validity: Amity University Online is UGC-entitled, so the online BBA is valid for jobs, higher studies, and government exams on the same terms as a regular degree from a UGC-approved university.
  • Learning format: Live online classes plus recorded lectures, so you can revise at your own pace and keep a part-time job or internship alongside.
  • Specialisation: The standard online BBA is a General BBA, covering the core business subjects — marketing, finance, HR, and operations — rather than a single narrow specialisation.

What the General BBA covers

The standard online BBA is a General BBA, which means you study the core business subjects — marketing, finance, human resources, and operations — instead of locking yourself into one narrow stream at 18. If you want a sharper industry angle, there is also a separate BBA with HCLTech in Data Analytics, which costs ₹2,25,000 in total (₹37,500 per semester) and adds an industry partner's name to your degree. Pick based on the work you actually enjoy — if you like numbers, lean towards Finance or Data Analytics later; if you like people and communication, Marketing or HR.

What the BBA fee covers — and what to check

The ₹1,65,000 total is the course fee for the standard online BBA. Before you pay anything, confirm three things in writing: the semester-wise breakup, whether any examination or admission fee applies on top, and the refund policy if you withdraw early. Fees can change between admission cycles, so treat the figure here as the fee listed in the counsellor guide and on FindRightCollege, and verify the final amount on the official website before paying.

Who should pick Amity's online BBA

  • Students who want a recognised brand name on their degree while studying from home.
  • Students planning BBA followed by MBA, because the BBA builds the base and the MBA adds the salary jump.
  • Students who want flexibility — recorded lectures mean you can intern, prepare for competitive exams, or help with family work alongside the degree.
  • Students on a mid-range budget — at ₹1,65,000 it sits between budget options like Dr. D.Y. Patil's BBA at ₹1,20,200 and premium options like Symbiosis at ₹1,65,000 and NMIMS at ₹1,31,000–₹1,45,000.

Amity BBA vs other online BBAs

  • Amity Online BBA: ₹1,65,000 total — big brand, 31 online programmes, industry-linked variants.
  • Manipal University Jaipur (MUJ) BBA: ₹1,35,000 total (₹22,500 per semester) — a lower fee from another well-known name.
  • Dr. D.Y. Patil BBA: ₹1,20,200 total — the lowest fee among the well-known private universities on this list.
  • LPU Online BBA: ₹1,22,400 total with the current 20% Student Grant (actual fee ₹1,50,000) — check whether the grant still applies when you apply.
  • JAIN Online BBA: ₹65,000–₹89,667 per year depending on the elective — the per-year format makes it easier to compare with annual budgets.

The cheapest option is not automatically the best one, and the most expensive is not either. Compare the fee with the specialisation you want, the learning support, and what you plan to do after the degree.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is the Amity online BBA valid for jobs?

Yes. Amity University Online is UGC-entitled, so its online BBA is a valid degree for private-sector jobs, higher studies, and government exams, on the same terms as a regular degree from a UGC-approved university. What matters after that is your skills, internships, and how you perform in interviews — the degree opens the door, it does not walk you through it.

What is the total fee of the Amity online BBA?

The total fee is ₹1,65,000, payable as ₹27,500 per semester over 3 years. A one-time discounted fee of ₹1,51,800 is also listed in the counsellor guide. Always confirm the final fee on the official website before admission, because fees can change between admission cycles.

Can I do an MBA after an online BBA from Amity?

Yes. An online BBA is a recognised bachelor's degree, so you are eligible to apply for an MBA — online or regular — after completing it. Many students follow the BBA-then-MBA route, and it works best when you pick your MBA specialisation based on the work experience or internships you gather during the BBA.

Is an online BBA better than a regular BBA?

Neither is better for everyone. A regular BBA gives you campus life, daily classroom discipline, and face-to-face networking. An online BBA gives you flexibility, lower overall cost, and the freedom to intern or work alongside your degree. If you need structure and a campus experience, go regular. If you need flexibility and affordability, online is a genuine option — not a compromise.

Ask Your Senior

I will say this plainly: a BBA is a starting line, not a finish line. Amity's online BBA at ₹1,65,000 is a fair, recognised option if you want a known brand and flexible study — but the degree alone will not get you a high-paying job. Use the three years to do internships, learn Excel and basic data skills, and build one real project you can talk about in interviews. That is what turns a BBA into a career.
