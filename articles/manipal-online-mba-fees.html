Manipal Online MBA Fees — MUJ and MAHE Fee Structures Compared

Manipal offers its online MBA through two universities with different fees. MAHE Manipal's MBA for Working Professionals costs ₹2,80,000 in total (₹70,000 per semester). For Manipal University Jaipur (MUJ), no MBA fee is quoted here, because the website and the counsellor guide list different totals for the same programme.

You searched "Manipal online MBA fees" and found two different numbers from two different Manipal universities — and maybe a third number from a random blog. No wonder you are confused. MUJ and MAHE are both Manipal, both UGC-entitled, and both genuine — but they are separate universities with separate fee structures. This guide separates them clearly, prints only the fee that is verified, and tells you honestly where the MUJ number stands.

MAHE Manipal — MBA for Working Professionals: ₹2,80,000

  • Total fee: ₹2,80,000 (₹70,000 per semester × 4 semesters).
  • Duration: 2 years (4 semesters).
  • Who it is for: Working professionals — the programme is designed around people who are already employed and want a management degree without leaving their job.
  • Degree validity: MAHE (Manipal Academy of Higher Education) is UGC-entitled, so the online MBA is valid for jobs, higher studies, and government exams on the same terms as a regular MBA from a UGC-approved university.
  • Other MAHE online programmes for comparison: MCA ₹2,20,000, MSc Data Science ₹2,80,000, MSc Business Analytics ₹2,80,000, and short PG Certificate programmes at ₹1,20,000–₹1,40,000.

MUJ — Manipal University Jaipur MBA: fee not quoted here

Here is the honest position. The FindRightCollege website lists one total for the MUJ online MBA, while the counsellor guide lists a different total for the same programme. The two figures disagree, and the rule on FindRightCollege is that a fee is printed only when the plan, the guide, and the website agree. They do not agree here, so no MUJ MBA fee appears in this article.

What you should do: ask MUJ directly for the current MBA fee in writing before you plan your budget. Do not rely on any blog — including this one — for this particular number until the two sources are reconciled.

MUJ fees that are verified

While the MBA fee is not quoted, these MUJ fees are confirmed across the guide and the website:

  • Online BBA: ₹1,35,000 total (₹22,500 per semester).
  • Online BCA: ₹1,35,000 total (₹22,500 per semester).
  • Online MCA: ₹1,58,000 total (₹39,500 per semester).
  • Online B.Com: ₹99,000 total (₹16,500 per semester).
  • Online M.Com: ₹1,08,000 total (₹27,000 per semester).
  • MA in Journalism and Mass Communication: ₹1,40,000 total (₹35,000 per semester).
  • MA in Economics: ₹80,000 total (₹20,000 per semester).

MUJ vs MAHE — which Manipal MBA should you pick?

  • Pick MAHE's MBA for Working Professionals (₹2,80,000) if you are already employed and want a programme built around working professionals, and the fee fits your budget.
  • Pick MUJ's MBA if you are a fresh graduate or early-career student and the Manipal name matters to you at a lower fee — but confirm the exact fee from MUJ first, as explained above.
  • Pick neither on brand alone. Both carry the Manipal name, but the programmes, the fees, and the target students are different. Compare the fee you actually confirm, the specialisations offered, and the learning format.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the fee of the Manipal online MBA?

It depends on which Manipal university you mean. MAHE Manipal's MBA for Working Professionals costs ₹2,80,000 in total (₹70,000 per semester). For MUJ (Manipal University Jaipur), the website and the counsellor guide list different totals for the MBA, so I am not quoting an MUJ MBA fee until the two sources agree — confirm it from MUJ directly.

Which is better, MUJ or MAHE for an online MBA?

Neither is better for everyone. MAHE's MBA is designed for working professionals and costs ₹2,80,000, while MUJ's MBA targets a broader audience including fresh graduates at a lower fee. Choose MAHE if you are employed and want a working-professional format; choose MUJ if you are early-career and the fee matters more. Confirm the MUJ fee from the university first.

Is the Manipal online MBA valid?

Yes. Both MUJ and MAHE are UGC-entitled universities, so their online MBAs are valid for private-sector jobs, higher studies, and government exams on the same terms as a regular MBA from a UGC-approved university. Verify the university's status on the UGC-DEB website yourself before paying.

Are there discounts on Manipal online MBA fees?

The counsellor guide notes that Manipal students can avail a coupon discount on weekdays and a 10% discount on overall fees, plus 5% on annual payment, without a discount coupon. Discounts change, so ask the university what applies to your admission cycle and get it in writing before you pay.
