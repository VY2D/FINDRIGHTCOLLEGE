Sikkim Manipal University Online MBA Admission Process — Step by Step

Sikkim Manipal University (SMU) admits students to its online MBA directly on graduation marks, with no entrance exam. The process is: check your eligibility, fill the online application, upload your documents, pay the fee, and complete verification. The online MBA with dual specialisation costs ₹1,10,000 for the full programme, payable in semester instalments of ₹27,500.

You have picked Sikkim Manipal for your online MBA — probably because the fee is among the lowest on FindRightCollege — and you want the admission process without the sales pitch. Here are the steps, the fee and the documents, in order.

Step 1: Check that you are eligible

SMU's online MBA is a postgraduate programme, so you need a bachelor's degree from a recognised university. Most programmes listed on FindRightCollege ask for 50% marks in graduation — confirm the exact requirement on the university's admission page before applying, because the requirement is set by the university.

There is no entrance exam. None of the online MBA brochures listed on FindRightCollege asks for a CAT, MAT or any other entrance-exam score, and SMU is no exception. Admission is based on your qualifying-exam marks.

Step 2: Choose your two specialisations

SMU's online MBA is a dual-specialisation programme — you study two specialisations in one degree, at no extra fee. The options are Marketing, Finance, HR, Systems, and Operations and Supply Chain Management. Pick a combination that matches the work you want to do: Marketing with Finance for brand and business roles, HR with Operations for people and process roles, or Systems with Operations for technology-led operations roles.

The dual specialisation is the real value of this programme. Most online MBAs give you one specialisation; SMU gives you two for ₹1,10,000. But two specialisations only help if both are subjects you can study properly — do not pick a second one at random just because it is free.

Step 3: Fill the online application

Apply on Sikkim Manipal University's official online admission portal. You will fill in your personal details, education history and your chosen specialisations. Keep these ready before you start:

  • Your graduation mark sheets and degree certificate (or provisional certificate if the final degree is still awaited)
  • Class 10 and Class 12 mark sheets
  • A government ID proof
  • A recent passport-size photograph

Fill in your own details and double-check your name, date of birth and email address. A wrong email means you will miss every update the university sends.

Step 4: Pay the fee

SMU's online MBA with dual specialisation costs ₹1,10,000 for the full programme, payable in semester instalments of ₹27,500 per semester over four semesters. That makes it one of the lowest-fee online MBAs on FindRightCollege — compare it with Amity at ₹1,99,000 or NMIMS at ₹1,96,000 before deciding.

Pay only through the university's official payment link or portal. Never transfer the fee to a personal account, an individual's account or a random UPI ID. If someone pressures you to pay immediately to "lock a seat," slow down — online MBA seats are not allotted like that.

Step 5: Document verification and enrolment

After you apply and pay, the university verifies your documents. Once verification is complete, you receive your enrolment confirmation and login details for the online learning platform. Your classes, study material, assignments and exams all run through that platform.

The online MBA is a two-year programme spread across four semesters. Before the first class, log in once, check that your subjects and both specialisations are correct, and confirm your exam schedule. If anything looks wrong, raise it with the university immediately.

Other courses at SMU

If an MBA is not the right fit yet, SMU also offers an online MCA at ₹98,000 for the full programme, and MA and M.Com at ₹79,000 each. The MCA is a strong budget option if you are from a tech background and want an IT career instead of a management one.

A note on validity

Sikkim Manipal University is a UGC-approved university with NAAC A+ accreditation, and its degrees are UGC-entitled. That is the condition that matters: only a UGC-approved university's online degree is treated as equal to a regular degree. Before you pay any fee, verify the university's UGC-DEB approval yourself — it takes two minutes and it is the single most important check in this whole process.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the fee for Sikkim Manipal University's online MBA?

The online MBA with dual specialisation at Sikkim Manipal University costs ₹1,10,000 for the full programme, payable in semester instalments of ₹27,500 per semester. You study two specialisations in one degree at this fee. Confirm the current fee on the university's official page before paying.

Is there an entrance exam for SMU's online MBA?

No. SMU's online MBA admits students directly on graduation marks, with no CAT, MAT or other entrance exam. This is the same for all online MBA programmes listed on FindRightCollege — admission is based on your qualifying-exam marks, not on an entrance-test score.

What is a dual specialisation MBA at SMU?

A dual specialisation MBA lets you study two specialisations in a single degree. At SMU you choose two from Marketing, Finance, HR, Systems, and Operations and Supply Chain Management. It is useful if you are torn between two fields, because you graduate with depth in both instead of one.

How long is SMU's online MBA?

It is a two-year programme spread across four semesters. You study online through the university's learning platform, with lectures, study material, assignments and online exams. The degree is awarded by Sikkim Manipal University, a UGC-approved university.

Ask Your Senior

SMU's MBA is genuinely good value — ₹1,10,000 for a dual specialisation from a NAAC A+ university is hard to beat. But value only matters if you finish the degree, so be honest with yourself before applying: can you give it two years of steady study? If yes, apply yourself, pay only on the official portal, and start with a clear plan for both your specialisations.
