LPU Online MBA Fees — Full Fee Structure Explained

LPU Online's MBA costs ₹1,61,600 in total with the current 20% Student Grant — or ₹2,00,000 without it. The fee is charged semester-wise: ₹40,400 per semester with the grant, against an actual semester fee of ₹50,000. The MBA is UGC-entitled, so it holds the same validity as a regular MBA.

You have probably seen LPU's MBA quoted as ₹1,61,600 everywhere — and it is a real figure, but only with the Student Grant applied. The grant is a discount, not the base price, and discounts can end. This guide shows you both numbers, explains how the grant works, and compares LPU honestly with the other online MBAs, so your budget is built on the full picture rather than the smaller number alone.

LPU Online MBA — the full fee structure

  • With the current 20% Student Grant: ₹1,61,600 total (₹40,400 per semester × 4 semesters).
  • Without the grant (actual fee): ₹2,00,000 total (₹50,000 per semester × 4 semesters).
  • Your saving with the grant: ₹38,400.
  • Examination fee: ₹2,000 per semester, charged separately.
  • Duration: 2 years (4 semesters), 102 credits.
  • Eligibility: Graduation in any discipline.
  • Degree validity: LPU (Lovely Professional University) is UGC-entitled, so the online MBA is valid for jobs, higher studies, and government exams on the same terms as a regular MBA from a UGC-approved university.

How the Student Grant works — and what to check

The 20% Student Grant is a reduction LPU currently applies to the actual fee. It is not a permanent price, and it is not guaranteed forever — grants change between admission cycles. Before you budget ₹1,61,600, ask LPU in writing whether the grant applies to your admission cycle and whether it covers all four semesters or only the first. If the grant does not apply, your fee is ₹2,00,000 plus examination fees, and you should plan for that figure. Also confirm the examination fee of ₹2,000 per semester in writing, because it sits outside the headline total.

Specialisations you can choose

LPU's online MBA offers specialisations in Human Resource Management, Finance, Marketing, Operations Management, Business Analytics, Digital Marketing, Data Science, Information Technology, International Business, Supply Chain and Logistics, Hospital and Healthcare Management, and Financial Markets. That is one of the widest lists among online MBAs — if you know the field you want, there is likely a specialisation for it. Pick the one that matches the job you want after the MBA, not the one that sounds most modern.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the fee of the LPU online MBA?

The total fee is ₹1,61,600 with the current 20% Student Grant, or ₹2,00,000 without it. The semester fee is ₹40,400 with the grant and ₹50,000 without it, and an examination fee of ₹2,000 per semester is charged separately. Always confirm whether the grant applies to your admission cycle before you budget.

What is the Student Grant in LPU's online MBA?

The Student Grant is a 20% reduction LPU currently applies to the actual MBA fee, bringing the total down from ₹2,00,000 to ₹1,61,600 — a saving of ₹38,400. It is a limited offer, not a permanent price, so ask LPU in writing whether it covers your admission cycle and all four semesters.

Is the LPU online MBA valid for jobs?

Yes. LPU is UGC-entitled, so its online MBA is valid for private-sector jobs, higher studies, and government exams on the same terms as a regular MBA from a UGC-approved university. Verify the university's status on the UGC-DEB website yourself before paying.

Which LPU online MBA specialisation is best?

It depends on your career goal. Finance and Business Analytics suit students who like numbers and data, Marketing and Digital Marketing suit students who like brands and communication, and HR suits students who like working with people. Hospital and Healthcare Management and Financial Markets suit students targeting those specific sectors. Choose the specialisation that matches the job you want after the MBA.
