What You Study in an Online BCA

An online BCA typically covers programming, data structures, database management systems, operating systems, computer networks, web development and software engineering, with projects in the later semesters. Specializations vary by university — MUJ, for example, offers Data Science & Analytics, Cloud Computing and Cyber Security, while Shoolini offers AI & ML, Cyber Security, Full-Stack Development and Data Science.

When you enquire about LPU's BCA, ask for the specialization list, the semester-wise subjects and the examination pattern in writing.

How Admission Works

Admission follows the standard online-degree steps: choose your university, course and specialization; check the eligibility and keep your documents ready; fill the application form; upload your documents; and pay the fee on the official payment portal. Your 10th and 12th mark sheets and certificates, a government photo ID, recent photographs and your signature are the usual documents. For LPU specifically, remember the ₹1,000 non-refundable registration fee comes first, at the registration step itself.

Pay only on the official portal, and keep every receipt. If anyone asks you to pay a "blocking amount" to a personal account, stop and ask us first.

Is an Online BCA from LPU Valid?

An online degree is treated at par with a regular degree only when the university is UGC-approved to offer that programme online. That is the condition, every time, for every university. Before paying, check LPU's UGC-DEB entitlement for the BCA programme on the UGC-DEB portal yourself. Validity is a fact you verify, not a promise you accept on a phone call.
