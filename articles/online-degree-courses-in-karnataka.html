Online Degree Courses in Karnataka — Universities You Can Study From

Two UGC-approved universities on FindRightCollege are based in Karnataka — JAIN Online in Bengaluru and MAHE (Manipal Academy of Higher Education) in Manipal. Both offer online degrees you can study from home, anywhere in India. JAIN's online MBA starts at ₹98,000 per year, while MAHE's online MBA for working professionals is ₹2,80,000.

You live in Karnataka, or you want a Karnataka university's name on your degree, and you are searching for which online courses actually exist there. The confusion is fair — dozens of websites list Karnataka colleges, but very few of them offer a full UGC-approved online degree you can complete from home. Here are the two that are on FindRightCollege, with their real courses and fees.

The two Karnataka universities on FindRightCollege

Only two of the 17 universities on FindRightCollege are based in Karnataka. That is a good thing, not a limitation — it means you can compare them properly instead of drowning in a list of fifty names.

  • JAIN Online (JAIN Deemed-to-be University, Bengaluru) — a Bengaluru-based university with one of the widest online course lists on the site: MBA, BBA, BCA, MCA, B.Com and M.Com.
  • MAHE (Manipal Academy of Higher Education, Manipal) — based in Manipal, Karnataka, and aimed more at working professionals, with an MBA for working professionals, MCA, MSc programmes and short PG certificates.

Both are UGC-approved universities, and that is the condition that matters: only a UGC-approved university's online degree is treated as equal to a regular degree. Before you pay any fee, check the university's UGC-DEB approval yourself.

What you can study at JAIN Online

JAIN charges per year, so read the fee as a yearly figure, not a total. Its online courses include:

  • Online MBA — ₹98,000 to ₹1,40,000 per year, depending on the specialisation you choose. Specialisations include Finance, Marketing, Human Resource Management and General Management.
  • Online BCA — ₹45,000 per year, with a Computer Science and Information Technology specialisation.
  • Online BBA — from ₹65,000 per year, with specialisations such as Human Resource Management, Finance and Marketing.
  • Online MCA — from ₹75,000 per year, including Computer Science and IT, Cyber Security and Data Analytics.

JAIN also lists B.Com and M.Com online. Because the fee is yearly, a two-year MBA at the base rate works out to ₹1,96,000 in total — always add up the years before comparing with a university that quotes a total programme fee.

What you can study at MAHE Manipal

MAHE quotes total programme fees, and its courses are built for people who are already working:

  • Online MBA for Working Professionals — ₹2,80,000 for the full programme.
  • Online MCA — ₹2,20,000 for the full programme.
  • MSc in Data Science — ₹2,80,000 for the full programme.
  • MSc in Business Analytics — ₹2,80,000 for the full programme.
  • PG Certificate Programmes (PGCP) — ₹1,20,000 to ₹1,40,000, including Logistics and Supply Chain Management and Business Analytics. These are shorter certificate programmes, not full degrees.

MAHE's MBA is the costlier option of the two Karnataka universities, and it is pitched at working professionals rather than fresh graduates. If you are a fresher on a budget, JAIN's base MBA at ₹98,000 per year is the more realistic starting point.

Does the university's location matter for an online degree?

No — and this is the part most students get wrong. In an online degree, you study from your own home. Lectures, assignments and exams happen online. The university being in Bengaluru or Manipal does not mean you have to travel there for classes.

Location matters in only two small ways. First, some universities hold an optional campus visit or induction — JAIN and MAHE both have campuses you can visit, but attendance is not the degree. Second, a known city name on your degree can feel reassuring in interviews, but recruiters check the university's approval and your skills, not its pin code. Choose on fees, specialisations and approval — not on the city.

How to choose between JAIN and MAHE

Ask yourself three questions. First, what is your budget — JAIN starts far lower per year, while MAHE's programmes are premium-priced for working professionals. Second, are you a fresher or already working — MAHE's MBA is designed for working professionals, while JAIN's wider UG and PG list suits freshers too. Third, which specialisation do you actually want — check the specialisation list before the brand name, because the wrong specialisation at a famous university is still the wrong course.

Frequently Asked Questions

Which universities offer online degrees in Karnataka?

On FindRightCollege, two Karnataka-based universities offer online degrees: JAIN Online in Bengaluru and MAHE in Manipal. JAIN offers MBA, BBA, BCA, MCA, B.Com and M.Com online, while MAHE offers an MBA for working professionals, MCA, MSc in Data Science and Business Analytics, and PG certificate programmes. Is an online degree from a Karnataka university valid in other states?

Yes, if the university is UGC-approved to offer online degrees. A UGC-approved online degree is valid across India, not only in the state where the university sits. You can live in Delhi, Jaipur or anywhere else and study online from JAIN or MAHE. Always verify the university's UGC-DEB approval before paying any fee.

What is the fee for an online MBA in Karnataka?

At JAIN Online, the online MBA costs ₹98,000 to ₹1,40,000 per year depending on specialisation. At MAHE, the online MBA for working professionals costs ₹2,80,000 for the full programme. JAIN quotes fees per year and MAHE quotes a total programme fee, so add up the years before comparing the two.

Can I study an online degree from Bangalore without living there?

Yes. Online degrees are studied from home — lectures, study material, assignments and exams are all online. You do not need to live in Bengaluru or Manipal to enrol in JAIN Online or MAHE. Some universities offer an optional campus visit, but it is not required to complete the degree.
