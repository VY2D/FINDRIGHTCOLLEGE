What You Study in an Online BCA

An online BCA typically covers programming, data structures, database management systems, operating systems, computer networks, web development and software engineering, with projects in the later semesters. Specializations vary by university — MUJ, for example, offers Data Science & Analytics, Cloud Computing and Cyber Security, while Shoolini offers AI & ML, Cyber Security, Full-Stack Development and Data Science.

When you enquire about CU's BCA, ask for the specialization list, the semester-wise subjects and the examination pattern in writing.

What We Do Know About CU's Fees

CU's MBA fee is verified and published: ₹1,58,000 with the Early Bird Discount, and ₹2,10,668 without it. The discount is worth ₹52,668, which is why CU's MBA articles always carry both figures with their labels. That is how a fee should be shown — with the condition attached, not hidden.

The BCA fee is a different matter. It is not in the counsellor guide, and it is not in the BCA fee list on the FindRightCollege website either. So this article lists the BCA fees we can verify, so you can still compare while you wait for CU's figure:

  • Shoolini University BCA: ₹78,000 total
  • Mizoram University BCA: ₹64,700 total
  • MUJ (Manipal University Jaipur) BCA: ₹1,35,000 total (₹22,500 per semester)
  • JAIN Online BCA: ₹45,000 per year (₹1,35,000 total), plus a ₹2,500 one-time registration fee and a ₹3,000 yearly examination fee
  • Amity University Online BCA: ₹1,50,000 total (₹25,000 per semester)
  • UPES Online BCA: ₹1,50,000 total
  • Jamia Hamdard BCA: ₹16,000 per semester

If CU's BCA fee comes back higher than Shoolini's ₹78,000, ask what the extra money buys. If it comes back lower, ask why. Either way, the fee alone never decides — the subjects, the exam pattern and the university's UGC entitlement decide with it.

How Admission Works

CU's application asks for an ABC ID (Academic Bank of Credits ID) and a DEB ID on the form, as per its brochure. The counsellor walks you through creating both, free. Beyond that, admission follows the standard steps: choose your university, course and specialization; check the eligibility and keep your documents ready; fill the application form; upload your documents; and pay the fee on the official payment portal. Your 10th and 12th mark sheets and certificates, a government photo ID, recent photographs and your signature are the usual documents.

Pay only on the official portal, and keep every receipt. If anyone asks you to pay a "blocking amount" to a personal account, stop and ask us first.

Is an Online BCA from Chandigarh University Valid?

An online degree is treated at par with a regular degree only when the university is UGC-approved to offer that programme online. That is the condition, every time, for every university. Before paying, check CU's UGC-DEB entitlement for the BCA programme on the UGC-DEB portal yourself. Validity is a fact you verify, not a promise you accept on a phone call.
