Online MBA Entrance Exam — Is There an Entrance Test for an Online MBA?

No, there is no entrance exam for an online MBA. None of the online MBA brochures listed on FindRightCollege asks for a CAT, MAT or any other entrance-exam score. Admission to an online MBA is based on your graduation marks — you apply directly to the university, upload your documents, pay the fee, and enrol.

You have been searching for the online MBA entrance exam because every MBA story you have heard starts with CAT. Friends preparing for CAT, coaching ads promising a top percentile, cut-offs, mock tests — it all makes an entrance exam feel like a compulsory first step. For an online MBA, it is not. This article explains why, and what replaces the exam.

Why full-time MBAs have entrance exams and online MBAs do not

A full-time MBA at a top institute has limited seats and thousands of applicants, so it uses an entrance exam to filter them. CAT, XAT and MAT exist to rank applicants when seats are scarce.

Online MBA programmes work differently. They are built for scale — lectures are recorded, learning happens on a platform, and there is no physical classroom with forty chairs. Because seats are not scarce in the same way, universities admit students on their qualifying-exam marks instead of an entrance-test score. Your graduation result is the filter, not a separate test.

What admission actually looks like

The online MBA admission process on FindRightCollege is the same across universities:

  1. Check eligibility. You need a bachelor's degree from a recognised university. Most programmes ask for 50% marks in graduation — confirm the exact requirement on the university's page.
  2. Choose your specialisation. Finance, Marketing, HR, Business Analytics, Operations and more, depending on the university.
  3. Fill the online application. Personal details, education history and your chosen programme.
  4. Upload documents. Graduation mark sheets and degree certificate, Class 10 and 12 mark sheets, a government ID proof and a photograph.
  5. Pay the fee. Only through the university's official portal, never to a personal account.
  6. Verification and enrolment. The university checks your documents and sends your login for the learning platform.

No test, no cut-off, no mock exams. If someone tells you that you must clear an entrance test for an online MBA, they are either confused with a full-time MBA or selling you coaching you do not need.

The fees you will actually pay

Since there is no exam fee, the only money involved is the programme fee. For reference, the lowest-fee online MBAs on FindRightCollege are Mizoram University at ₹50,700 to ₹53,100 and Sikkim Manipal University at ₹1,10,000 for a dual specialisation. Mid-range options include Dr. D.Y. Patil at ₹1,89,400, NMIMS at ₹1,96,000 and Amity at ₹1,99,000. UPES charges ₹1,75,000 to ₹2,20,000 depending on specialisation, and MAHE's MBA for working professionals is ₹2,80,000. JAIN Online charges ₹98,000 to ₹1,40,000 per year. Always confirm the current fee on the university's official page before paying.

The one exception to know about

There is one situation where an entrance exam still matters: if you later apply for a full-time MBA at an institute that requires CAT or a similar test, you will need to prepare for it separately. An online MBA does not exempt you from that, and it does not replace a CAT score. But that is a different goal. For the online MBA itself, no entrance exam exists in the process.

Similarly, some universities may ask for an entrance score for their full-time or executive programmes — NMIMS, for example, offers an Executive MBA (WX) at ₹4,00,000 aimed at senior leaders. Do not confuse the requirements of one programme with another. Check the requirements of the exact programme you are applying to.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I do an online MBA without an entrance exam?

Yes. None of the online MBA brochures listed on FindRightCollege requires a CAT, MAT or any other entrance-exam score. Admission is based on your graduation marks. You apply directly to the university, upload your documents, pay the fee and enrol. This is different from many full-time MBA programmes, where an entrance score is required.

Do I need CAT for an online MBA?

No. CAT is an entrance test for full-time MBA programmes at institutes that use it for selection. Online MBA programmes on FindRightCollege do not ask for a CAT score at any stage. If a coaching institute or agent tells you CAT is needed for an online MBA, that information is wrong.

Is there any test before online MBA admission?

No test is part of the admission process for the online MBA programmes listed on FindRightCollege. The university checks your eligibility and verifies your documents. Some universities may hold a short interaction or guidance call, but that is guidance, not an examination, and it does not decide your admission.

Does an online MBA without an entrance exam have the same value?

The value of an online MBA depends on the university's UGC approval, not on whether there was an entrance exam. Only a UGC-approved university's online degree is treated as equal to a regular degree. Check the university's UGC-DEB approval before paying any fee — that approval is what makes the degree valid, not a test score.
