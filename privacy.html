Privacy Policy

How FindRightCollege collects, uses and protects your information.

What We Collect

When you fill in our enquiry form, we collect only the details needed to guide you on admission: your name, email, mobile, date of birth (DOB), and city/state. We do not ask for your Aadhaar number — only the city printed on it, so your admission documents match.

How We Use Your Information

The information you share is used only for admission guidance — to understand the course, university and specialization you are interested in, and to help you with the admission process. We never sell your information, and we never share it with third parties.

How We Share Your Details

When you submit the enquiry form, your details open in WhatsApp, ready to send to our counsellor on +91 72360 32906 — only if you choose to send the message. We do not pass your details to universities, agents or any other third party.

Correction and Deletion

You can ask us to correct or delete your information at any time. Write to us at findrightdegreeonline@gmail.com and we will correct or delete your details.
