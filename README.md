## How admission works here

1. Fill the enquiry form or WhatsApp us
2. Get free guidance from a senior — compare fees, eligibility and universities honestly
3. Apply through the **university's own official admission process**

> FindRightCollege is a guidance platform. It does **not** admit students, collect fees, or guarantee admission or placement. All admissions happen only through the respective universities' official channels.
