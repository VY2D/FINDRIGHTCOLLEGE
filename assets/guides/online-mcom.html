Online M.Com Admission — Fees, Eligibility & Process After Graduation

An online M.Com (Master of Commerce) is a 2-year postgraduate commerce degree done fully online after graduation. This guide covers online M.Com admission, fees and eligibility from the actual brochures listed on FindRightCollege.

📌 Please note: FindRightCollege is an admission guidance consultancy — this is not an official university website. Fees and details are taken from the universities' brochures; please confirm the final fee and eligibility on the official university website before admission.

Online M.Com Admission Process — Step by Step

  1. Choose a UGC-entitled university. Shortlist 2–3 universities offering the Online M.Com online and compare total fee, specializations, NAAC grade and student support. Only apply to a university that is entitled by the UGC to offer online programmes — verify this on the university's official website or the UGC-DEB portal.

  2. Check your eligibility. Read the eligibility for your course carefully (marks and qualifying exam) before you apply, so your application is not rejected later.

  3. Fill the application form. Fill the enquiry form below — University → Course → Specialization — and our college senior will confirm the exact admission steps, current fee and last date for your chosen university, then guide you to the official application.

  4. Upload your documents. Keep scanned copies of your mark sheets, certificates, photo ID, photograph and signature ready in the size and format the university asks for.

  5. Pay the fee. Pay only through the university's official payment link or portal — semester-wise, annually or in full, as the university allows. Never pay an admission fee into a personal account.

  6. Verification and enrolment. The university verifies your documents and eligibility. On approval you receive your enrolment/roll number and LMS (learning portal) login — your classes, study material, assignments and exams all happen through it.

Online M.Com Eligibility & Duration

Duration: 2 years. Eligibility (JAIN Online, as per brochure): a bachelor's degree from a recognized university; our college senior confirms the exact criteria for your chosen elective when you enquire. Amity University Online's M.Com is listed in the official brochures and university pages at ₹1,20,000 total.

Online M.Com Fees

As per the JAIN brochure: Professional Accounting & Finance (CPA, US) — ₹30,500/year; Accounting & Finance — ₹65,000/year; International Finance (accredited by ACCA, UK) — ₹1,30,000/year, plus a one-time ₹2,500 registration fee and ₹3,000/year examination fee. One elective — International Finance & Accounting (ACCA, UK) — appears in neither the brochure nor the guide, so it is marked fee on enquiry rather than guessed. Amity M.Com — ₹1,20,000 total (₹30,000 per semester), as per the official brochures and university pages. Also listed on the site: MUJ M.Com (₹1,08,000), SMU M.Com (₹79,000) and Mizoram University M.Com (₹43,200 total); AMU's M.Com is fee on enquiry because the guide's AMU fee table was not printed clearly.

Documents Required for Online M.Com Admission

  • 10th mark sheet and certificate — for date-of-birth proof and name spelling
  • 12th mark sheet and certificate
  • Graduation mark sheets (all semesters/years) and degree/provisional certificate
  • A government photo ID (Aadhaar card / PAN / passport) — for identity verification by the university
  • Recent passport-size photographs and a scanned signature
  • Migration certificate — only if your university asks for it
  • Category certificate (SC/ST/OBC/PwD) — only if you are claiming a marks relaxation or concession

The exact list and file format differ slightly by university — our college senior confirms the final list for your course before you apply. Note: the FindRightCollege enquiry form never asks for your Aadhaar number — only the city printed on it.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I take admission in an online M.Com?
Choose a UGC-entitled university, check the graduation eligibility, fill the application form, upload documents, pay the fee on the official portal and complete verification for enrolment and LMS access.
What are the fees for an online M.Com?
JAIN Online M.Com: ₹30,500/year for Professional Accounting & Finance (CPA, US), ₹65,000/year for Accounting & Finance and ₹1,30,000/year for International Finance (ACCA, UK), plus ₹2,500 registration and ₹3,000/year exam fee. One JAIN elective (International Finance & Accounting, ACCA, UK) and AMU's M.Com are fee on enquiry. Amity M.Com: ₹1,20,000 total as per the official brochures and university pages; also listed: MUJ ₹1,08,000, SMU ₹79,000, Mizoram University ₹43,200 total.
What is the eligibility for an online M.Com?
A bachelor's degree from a recognized university, as per the JAIN Online brochure. Our college senior confirms the exact criteria for your elective on enquiry.
How long is an online M.Com?
2 years.
