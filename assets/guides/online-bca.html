Online BCA Admission — Process, Fees & Eligibility After 12th

An online BCA (Bachelor of Computer Applications) is a 3-year undergraduate degree in computer science and IT, done fully online after Class 12. This guide covers the online BCA admission process, fees and eligibility from the actual brochures of the programmes listed on FindRightCollege.

📌 Please note: FindRightCollege is an admission guidance consultancy — this is not an official university website. Fees and details are taken from the universities' brochures; please confirm the final fee and eligibility on the official university website before admission.

Online BCA Admission Process — Step by Step

  1. Choose a UGC-entitled university. Shortlist 2–3 universities offering the Online BCA online and compare total fee, specializations, NAAC grade and student support. Only apply to a university that is entitled by the UGC to offer online programmes — verify this on the university's official website or the UGC-DEB portal.

  2. Check your eligibility. Read the eligibility for your course carefully (marks and qualifying exam) before you apply, so your application is not rejected later.

  3. Fill the application form. Fill the enquiry form below — University → Course → Specialization — and our counsellor will confirm the exact admission steps, current fee and last date for your chosen university, then guide you to the official application.

  4. Upload your documents. Keep scanned copies of your mark sheets, certificates, photo ID, photograph and signature ready in the size and format the university asks for.

  5. Pay the fee. Pay only through the university's official payment link or portal — semester-wise, annually or in full, as the university allows. Never pay an admission fee into a personal account.

  6. Verification and enrolment. The university verifies your documents and eligibility. On approval you receive your enrolment/roll number and LMS (learning portal) login — your classes, study material, assignments and exams all happen through it.

Online BCA Eligibility & Duration

Duration: 3 years. Eligibility (JAIN Online, as per brochure): a pass in the 10+2 examination from a board recognized by the State or Central Government (State Board / CBSE / NIOS / IGCSE / IB / ICSE); candidates due to appear in 10+2 are also eligible to apply. The specialization listed is Computer Science & Information Technology. Amity University Online's BCA is listed in the counsellor guide at ₹1,50,000 total; its exact eligibility is confirmed from the official source when you enquire.

Online BCA Fees

JAIN Online BCA — ₹45,000 per year, plus a one-time university registration fee of ₹2,500 and a yearly examination fee of ₹3,000 (as per the JAIN brochure). Amity University Online BCA — ₹1,50,000 total (₹25,000 per semester), as per the counsellor guide. Also listed on the site: MUJ BCA (₹1,35,000), Shoolini BCA (₹78,000), UPES BCA (₹1,50,000), Jamia Hamdard BCA (₹16,000 per semester) and Mizoram University BCA (₹64,700 total).

Documents Required for Online BCA Admission

  • 10th mark sheet and certificate — for date-of-birth proof and name spelling
  • 12th mark sheet and certificate
  • A government photo ID (Aadhaar card / PAN / passport) — for identity verification by the university
  • Recent passport-size photographs and a scanned signature
  • Transfer certificate / migration certificate — only if your university asks for it
  • Category certificate (SC/ST/OBC/PwD) — only if you are claiming a marks relaxation or concession

The exact list and file format differ slightly by university — our counsellor confirms the final list for your course before you apply. Note: the FindRightCollege enquiry form never asks for your Aadhaar number — only the city printed on it.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the online BCA admission process?
Choose a UGC-entitled university, check the 10+2 eligibility, fill the application form, upload documents, pay the fee on the official portal and complete verification to receive enrolment and LMS access.
What is the eligibility for an online BCA?
A pass in 10+2 from a recognized board, as per the JAIN Online brochure. Students due to appear in their 10+2 examination are also eligible to apply.
What are the fees for an online BCA?
JAIN Online BCA costs ₹45,000 per year plus a ₹2,500 one-time registration fee and ₹3,000/year examination fee. Amity's online BCA costs ₹1,50,000 total as per the counsellor guide; MUJ, Shoolini, UPES, Jamia Hamdard and Mizoram University BCAs are also listed on the site.
Is maths compulsory for an online BCA?
The JAIN brochure's eligibility for BCA states a 10+2 pass from a recognized board and does not list a separate maths condition. Confirm the current rule for your chosen university when you enquire.
