UGC Approved Online Degree — What It Means & How to Verify

"UGC approved" is the most searched — and most misunderstood — phrase in online admissions. This guide explains, in original FindRightCollege words, what UGC entitlement means for an online degree, how to verify it yourself, and what to check before you pay any fee.

📌 Please note: FindRightCollege is an admission guidance consultancy — this is not an official university website. Fees and details are taken from the universities' brochures; please confirm the final fee and eligibility on the official university website before admission.

What Does 'UGC Approved Online Degree' Mean?

A university in India can offer online programmes only if it is entitled by the University Grants Commission (UGC) under its Open and Distance Learning / Online regulations, administered through the UGC's Distance Education Bureau (DEB). Universities generally qualify through their accreditation standing (for example, a strong NAAC grade or NIRF ranking, as the regulations prescribe). An online degree from an entitled university follows an approved curriculum, is examined by the university itself, and is treated at par with a regular degree of the same university.

Every programme on FindRightCollege is described exactly as its brochure states — for example, the brochures describe the online MBAs of Chandigarh University Online, Manipal University Jaipur, NMIMS CDOE, Amity University Online and JAIN Online as UGC-entitled, with NAAC grades from A+ to A++. We still ask you to verify independently, because entitlement is university- and programme-specific and can change.

How to Verify a University Before Admission

  1. Check the official university website for its UGC entitlement letter/approval for online programmes and the specific course you want.

  2. Check the UGC-DEB portal — the UGC publishes lists of entitled universities and approved programmes; search your university and programme there.

  3. Check accreditation — confirm the NAAC grade the brochure claims, on the NAAC or university website.

  4. Confirm the fee on the official site and pay only through the university's official payment channel.

  5. Save everything — brochure, fee receipt, enrolment email and your student ID.

Is an Online Degree Valid for Jobs and Further Study?

An online degree from a UGC-entitled university is a recognized Indian degree: it can be used for private-sector jobs, for government-job applications where the notification asks for a degree from a recognized university, and for further study such as an MBA after a UG degree or research programmes later. What an honest counsellor will never promise is a job or a salary — no degree, online or regular, can guarantee placement. Judge a programme by its university, curriculum and your own consistency in completing it.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is a UGC approved online degree?
It is an online degree offered by a university that the UGC has entitled to run online programmes under its ODL/Online regulations. Such degrees are treated at par with the same university's regular degrees.
How can I check whether an online degree is UGC approved?
Verify the university's entitlement on its official website and on the UGC-DEB portal's published lists, confirm its NAAC grade, and check that your exact programme is covered before paying any fee.
Is an online degree valid for government jobs?
An online degree from a UGC-entitled university is a recognized degree and can be used where a job notification requires a degree from a recognized university. Always read the specific notification's rules as well.
Does 'online' get printed on the degree certificate?
Practice differs by university. Ask the university directly — or ask our counsellor to confirm it for your chosen university before you apply.
