Online BBA Admission — Process, Fees & Eligibility After 12th

An online BBA (Bachelor of Business Administration) is a 3-year undergraduate business degree you complete over the internet after Class 12. This guide explains the online BBA admission process, eligibility, fees and documents using the actual brochure details of the online BBA programmes listed on FindRightCollege.

📌 Please note: FindRightCollege is an admission guidance consultancy — this is not an official university website. Fees and details are taken from the universities' brochures; please confirm the final fee and eligibility on the official university website before admission.

Online BBA Admission Process — Step by Step

  1. Choose a UGC-entitled university. Shortlist 2–3 universities offering the Online BBA online and compare total fee, specializations, NAAC grade and student support. Only apply to a university that is entitled by the UGC to offer online programmes — verify this on the university's official website or the UGC-DEB portal.

  2. Check your eligibility. Read the eligibility for your course carefully (marks and qualifying exam) before you apply, so your application is not rejected later.

  3. Fill the application form. Fill the enquiry form below — University → Course → Specialization — and our college senior will confirm the exact admission steps, current fee and last date for your chosen university, then guide you to the official application.

  4. Upload your documents. Keep scanned copies of your mark sheets, certificates, photo ID, photograph and signature ready in the size and format the university asks for.

  5. Pay the fee. Pay only through the university's official payment link or portal — semester-wise, annually or in full, as the university allows. Never pay an admission fee into a personal account.

  6. Verification and enrolment. The university verifies your documents and eligibility. On approval you receive your enrolment/roll number and LMS (learning portal) login — your classes, study material, assignments and exams all happen through it.

Online BBA Eligibility & Duration

Duration: 3 years. JAIN Online's BBA runs over 3 years and Shoolini's Power BBA runs 3 years (6 semesters). Eligibility: a pass in the 10+2 examination from a recognized board (State Board / CBSE / NIOS / IGCSE / IB / ICSE), as per the JAIN brochure; students due to appear in their 10+2 examination can also apply. For Shoolini Power BBA and Amity BBA, our college senior confirms the exact criteria from the official source when you enquire — we do not guess eligibility.

Online BBA Fees

As per the brochures: JAIN Online BBA — ₹65,000/year for HRM, Finance, Marketing and Engineering & Project Management electives, ₹89,667/year for Digital Marketing and Data Science & Analytics, plus a one-time ₹2,500 registration fee and ₹3,000/year examination fee. Shoolini Power BBA — fee on enquiry (check with us on WhatsApp). Amity University Online BBA — ₹1,65,000 total (₹27,500 per semester, as per the official brochures and university pages). More online BBAs from the official brochures and university pages are also listed on the site — MUJ (₹1,35,000), NMIMS (₹1,31,000–₹1,45,000), Dr. D.Y. Patil Pune (₹1,20,200), UPES (₹1,50,000), Mizoram University (₹55,700) and Jamia Hamdard (₹14,000 per semester). See the course pages for full details.

Documents Required for Online BBA Admission

  • 10th mark sheet and certificate — for date-of-birth proof and name spelling
  • 12th mark sheet and certificate
  • A government photo ID (Aadhaar card / PAN / passport) — for identity verification by the university
  • Recent passport-size photographs and a scanned signature
  • Transfer certificate / migration certificate — only if your university asks for it
  • Category certificate (SC/ST/OBC/PwD) — only if you are claiming a marks relaxation or concession

The exact list and file format differ slightly by university — our college senior confirms the final list for your course before you apply. Note: the FindRightCollege enquiry form never asks for your Aadhaar number — only the city printed on it.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the online BBA admission process?
Choose a UGC-entitled university, check the 10+2 eligibility, fill the application form, upload your 10th/12th documents, photo ID, photograph and signature, pay the fee on the university's official portal, and complete verification to get your enrolment and LMS login.
What is the eligibility for an online BBA?
A pass in 10+2 from a recognized board, as per the JAIN Online brochure; students appearing in 10+2 can also apply. There is no entrance exam for the programmes listed on this site.
What are the fees for an online BBA?
JAIN Online: ₹65,000–₹89,667 per year depending on the elective, plus ₹2,500 registration and ₹3,000/year exam fee. Shoolini Power BBA: fee on enquiry — check with us on WhatsApp. Amity BBA: ₹1,65,000 total, as per the official brochures and university pages. Also listed: MUJ ₹1,35,000, NMIMS ₹1,31,000–₹1,45,000, Dr. D.Y. Patil Pune ₹1,20,200, UPES ₹1,50,000, Mizoram University ₹55,700, Jamia Hamdard ₹14,000 per semester.
How long is an online BBA?
3 years — 6 semesters at Shoolini Power BBA, and 3 academic years at JAIN Online.
