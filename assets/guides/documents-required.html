Documents Required for Online Degree Admission — Full Checklist

Admission delays usually happen for one reason: documents. This checklist covers what universities generally ask for when you apply to an online MBA, BBA, BCA, MCA, B.Com or M.Com — so you can keep everything ready before you fill the form.

📌 Please note: FindRightCollege is an admission guidance consultancy — this is not an official university website. Fees and details are taken from the universities' brochures; please confirm the final fee and eligibility on the official university website before admission.

Documents for UG Admission (BBA, BCA, B.Com, BA)

  • 10th mark sheet and certificate — for date-of-birth proof and name spelling
  • 12th mark sheet and certificate
  • A government photo ID (Aadhaar card / PAN / passport) — for identity verification by the university
  • Recent passport-size photographs and a scanned signature
  • Transfer certificate / migration certificate — only if your university asks for it
  • Category certificate (SC/ST/OBC/PwD) — only if you are claiming a marks relaxation or concession

The exact list and file format differ slightly by university — our counsellor confirms the final list for your course before you apply. Note: the FindRightCollege enquiry form never asks for your Aadhaar number — only the city printed on it.

Documents for PG Admission (MBA, MCA, M.Com, MA)

  • 10th mark sheet and certificate — for date-of-birth proof and name spelling
  • 12th mark sheet and certificate
  • Graduation mark sheets (all semesters/years) and degree/provisional certificate
  • A government photo ID (Aadhaar card / PAN / passport) — for identity verification by the university
  • Recent passport-size photographs and a scanned signature
  • Migration certificate — only if your university asks for it
  • Category certificate (SC/ST/OBC/PwD) — only if you are claiming a marks relaxation or concession

The exact list and file format differ slightly by university — our counsellor confirms the final list for your course before you apply. Note: the FindRightCollege enquiry form never asks for your Aadhaar number — only the city printed on it.

Mistakes That Delay Verification

  • Name mismatch — your name should match across your 10th certificate, ID and application. If it does not, tell the counsellor before applying.
  • Blurry scans — scan in good light; every mark and seal should be readable.
  • Missing final-semester mark sheet — PG applicants in their final semester should keep all previous semester mark sheets ready.
  • Wrong city in documents — your admission documents should match your Aadhaar card details; that is why our enquiry form asks for your city exactly as per your Aadhaar card (never the Aadhaar number).

Keep digital copies in one folder before you start any application — the whole process then takes minutes, not days. When you enquire through FindRightCollege, our counsellor confirms the exact list for your university so nothing is missed.

Frequently Asked Questions

Which documents are required for online MBA admission?
Graduation mark sheets and degree/provisional certificate, 10th and 12th mark sheets, a government photo ID, passport-size photographs and a scanned signature. A migration or category certificate is needed only if your university asks or you claim a concession.
Which documents are required for online UG admission?
10th and 12th mark sheets and certificates, a government photo ID, photographs and a signature; a transfer/migration certificate only if the university asks for it.
Do I need to send original documents?
Online admissions generally work on scanned uploads, with the university verifying them digitally. Some universities may ask to see originals at an exam centre or during a later verification — follow your university's instructions and never hand originals to an unofficial agent.
Does FindRightCollege ask for my Aadhaar number?
No. Our form asks only for the city printed on your Aadhaar card, so your admission documents match. We never ask for your Aadhaar number.
