Online MBA Admission in India — Complete Step-by-Step Guide

Searching "how to do an online MBA" or "how to do MBA online"? This guide covers the entire online MBA admission process in India — eligibility, total fees, admission without an entrance exam, documents required and degree validity — using the actual brochure and guide details of the online MBA programmes listed on FindRightCollege.

📌 Please note: FindRightCollege is an admission guidance consultancy — this is not an official university website. Fees and details are taken from the universities' brochures; please confirm the final fee and eligibility on the official university website before admission.

Online MBA Admission Process — Step by Step

Admission to an online MBA follows the same six steps at almost every UGC-entitled university:

  1. Choose a UGC-entitled university. Shortlist 2–3 universities offering the Online MBA online and compare total fee, specializations, NAAC grade and student support. Only apply to a university that is entitled by the UGC to offer online programmes — verify this on the university's official website or the UGC-DEB portal.

  2. Check your eligibility. Read the eligibility for your course carefully (marks and qualifying exam) before you apply, so your application is not rejected later.

  3. Fill the application form. Fill the enquiry form below — University → Course → Specialization — and our counsellor will confirm the exact admission steps, current fee and last date for your chosen university, then guide you to the official application.

  4. Upload your documents. Keep scanned copies of your mark sheets, certificates, photo ID, photograph and signature ready in the size and format the university asks for.

  5. Pay the fee. Pay only through the university's official payment link or portal — semester-wise, annually or in full, as the university allows. Never pay an admission fee into a personal account.

  6. Verification and enrolment. The university verifies your documents and eligibility. On approval you receive your enrolment/roll number and LMS (learning portal) login — your classes, study material, assignments and exams all happen through it.

Online MBA Eligibility

You need a bachelor's degree in any discipline from a recognized university. As per the brochures on this site: Manipal University Jaipur and NMIMS CDOE ask for a minimum of 50% aggregate marks (45% for reserved categories at MUJ; 45% for SC/ST/OBC/PwD at NMIMS), JAIN Online asks for 50% (5% relaxation for SC/ST) and also accepts final-semester students, Chandigarh University Online accepts any recognized bachelor's degree including professional qualifications like CA/ICWA, and Amity's healthcare MBA accepts graduation with 40% (with a test option below 40%). Work experience is not compulsory for these regular MBA programmes — the executive and working-professional MBAs (NMIMS MBA (WX), MAHE and Dr. D.Y. Patil) do require work experience, as stated on their course pages.

Online MBA Fees in India

On this site, total online MBA fees from the brochures range from ₹1,50,000 (Shoolini Power MBA, discounted) and ₹1,58,000 (Chandigarh University Online, with Early Bird Discount; ₹2,10,668 without) to ₹1,80,000 (Manipal University Jaipur, plus a ₹500 application fee) and ₹1,96,000 (NMIMS CDOE, plus a ₹1,200 processing fee). JAIN Online charges ₹98,000–₹1,40,000 per year depending on the specialization, plus a ₹2,500 registration fee and a ₹3,000/year examination fee. Amity's Hospital & Healthcare MBA brochure shows a zero-cost EMI of ₹13,023/month without stating a clear total — we confirm it on enquiry instead of guessing. Compare every fee in the fee table or on each course page below.

Online MBA Without Entrance Exam

None of the online MBA brochures on this site asks for CAT, MAT or any other entrance-exam score. Admission is based on your graduation marks meeting the eligibility above. Amity's brochure mentions an eligibility test only for candidates below 40% in graduation. Always confirm the current rule for your university when you enquire.

Documents Required for Online MBA Admission

  • 10th mark sheet and certificate — for date-of-birth proof and name spelling
  • 12th mark sheet and certificate
  • Graduation mark sheets (all semesters/years) and degree/provisional certificate
  • A government photo ID (Aadhaar card / PAN / passport) — for identity verification by the university
  • Recent passport-size photographs and a scanned signature
  • Migration certificate — only if your university asks for it
  • Category certificate (SC/ST/OBC/PwD) — only if you are claiming a marks relaxation or concession

The exact list and file format differ slightly by university — our counsellor confirms the final list for your course before you apply. Note: the FindRightCollege enquiry form never asks for your Aadhaar number — only the city printed on it.

Is an Online MBA Valid in India?

Yes — an online MBA from a UGC-entitled university is valid for private jobs, government-job eligibility where a recognized degree is required, and further study, under UGC's online programme regulations, which treat entitled online degrees at par with regular degrees. What matters is the university's entitlement, not the word "online" — read our full guide on UGC approved online degrees and the online vs regular degree comparison.

Which Is the Best University for Online MBA?

There is no single best university — the right choice depends on your budget, the specialization you want and the brand that helps your career. A practical way to decide: fix your total budget first (online MBA fees on this site run from ₹50,700 (Mizoram University, total fee) to ₹4,00,000 (the NMIMS executive MBA – WX)), shortlist the specializations you actually want (hundreds of specializations are listed across the 17 universities here), check the NAAC grade (A++ at NMIMS and JAIN, A+ at CU and MUJ, as per their brochures), and then compare semester-wise payment options. Frequently Asked Questions

How to do an online MBA — how do I do an MBA online?
Pick a UGC-entitled university, confirm you have a bachelor's degree with the required marks (usually 50%), fill the application form, upload your documents, pay the fee through the university's official portal and complete verification. You then get an enrolment number and LMS login, and your classes, assignments and exams happen online over 2 years (4 semesters).
What is the online MBA admission process?
Six steps: (1) choose a UGC-entitled university, (2) check eligibility, (3) fill the application form, (4) upload documents, (5) pay the fee, and (6) complete document verification to receive your enrolment and LMS access. FindRightCollege guides you through each step for free.
What is the eligibility for an online MBA?
A bachelor's degree in any discipline from a recognized university. Most universities on this site ask for 50% aggregate marks (45% for reserved categories); Amity's healthcare MBA accepts 40% with a test option below that. No work experience is required for the regular MBA programmes listed here; the executive / working-professional MBAs do ask for work experience, as stated on their course pages.
What are the online MBA fees in India?
Among the brochure-listed programmes here, total fees range from ₹50,700 (Mizoram University) to ₹4,00,000 (NMIMS executive MBA – WX); the original brochure MBAs run from ₹1,50,000 (Shoolini Power MBA, discounted) to ₹2,10,668 (Chandigarh University Online without Early Bird Discount). JAIN Online charges ₹98,000–₹1,40,000 per year by specialization. Application, registration and exam fees are extra where the brochure states them.
Can I do an online MBA without an entrance exam?
Yes. None of the online MBA brochures listed on FindRightCollege requires CAT/MAT or another entrance score — admission is on graduation marks. Confirm the current rule for your chosen university at the time of admission.
Is an online MBA valid in India?
Yes, if the university is UGC-entitled to offer online programmes. UGC regulations treat such online degrees at par with regular degrees. Verify the entitlement on the university's official website or the UGC-DEB portal before paying.
